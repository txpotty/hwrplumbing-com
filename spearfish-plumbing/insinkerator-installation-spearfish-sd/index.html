    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Spearfish

    A standard install goes into a sink that already has a drain and power. We take the old disposal down, scrape the old putty off the flange and set a new flange with fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. The unit hangs on the ring, locks with a quarter turn, and the discharge tube meets the trap on a new slip-joint washer. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Badger Disposal Install in Spearfish

    Most replacement disposals in local kitchens are a Badger. These 1/3 to 3/4 HP units handle everyday scraps and fit nearly any standard sink base. The unit hangs on its quick-lock mount, the discharge tube goes on, a cord kit gets added if needed and we finish with a cold-water test grind. About an hour, usually.

    Evolution Series Installation in Spearfish

    Evolution units grind in two or three stages and carry extra sound insulation around the motor housing, which is why they sit taller and weigh more than a Badger. Clearance under the bowl matters here. We measure from the sink bottom to the trap arm before the install so the unit does not crowd the trap. The Compact fits tight cabinets, and the Excel needs the most room.

    Evolution Disposal Upgrades in Spearfish, SD
