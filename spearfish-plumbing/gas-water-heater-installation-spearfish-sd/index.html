    Direct Vent Gas Water Heater Installation in Spearfish

    Direct vent heaters pull combustion air from outside and send exhaust out through a sealed, concentric vent. That sealed setup is a good fit for tight utility rooms and interior closets, since the heater never draws air from the living space. Most run without electricity. The vent goes through a wall with proper clearances.

    Gas water heater installs usually require a permit. South Dakota bases its plumbing rules on the Uniform Plumbing Code, and the state licenses plumbers and oversees plumbing inspections. HWR Plumbing handles the permit and the inspection in Spearfish.

    Gas usually recovers faster and can cost less to run where gas rates are reasonable. Electric skips the vent and the gas line.
