    Softener Systems We Replace in Spearfish, SD

    Clack Water Softener Replacement in Spearfish

    Clack WS1 valves are easy to program and easy to rebuild, which is why a lot of water treatment shops have switched to them. We can replace just the valve on a good tank. Or the whole system.

    Brine Tank Replacement in Spearfish

    Salt bridges, crusty layers of salt that form over a hollow, stop the brine from making contact with the water and the softener quits softening. We break up the crust and scoop out the mushy salt underneath. We replace cracked tanks and bad safety floats. Pellets bridge less than rock salt.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Spearfish

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico's version uses water pressure, not power, to switch tanks. The price is higher.

    Replacing Pelican Water Systems in Spearfish

    Pelican systems usually combine a carbon filter with either a salt-based softener or a salt-free conditioner in one setup on the main line. We replace the media or the whole system, depending on age. Following the install specs keeps the warranty intact.

    Salt-Based Softener Swaps in Spearfish, SD

    Swapping a salt-based softener in South Dakota homes means disconnecting the old resin and brine tanks, cutting in new fittings if the loop is corroded, and setting the new valve. We pressurize and check every joint. We set an air gap on the drain line.

    Fleck Valve Programming in Spearfish, SD

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. The valve needs your hardness in grains, the number of people and the regeneration time set correctly, or it will regenerate too often or not enough. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Spearfish

    Not every softener failure is urgent. A unit leaking from the valve body or the brine tank overflowing onto the floor is, and we move those calls up the schedule. Turn the bypass handle and wait for us.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Spearfish, SD

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. Put the unit in bypass first. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.
