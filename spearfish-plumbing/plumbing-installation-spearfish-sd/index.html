    PEX Repiping in Spearfish

    Copper repipes cost more and take longer, which is why most whole-house repipes today use PEX-A or PEX-B tubing with brass or poly fittings. You also hear less of it, since PEX softens water hammer. It also handles a freeze better. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a much bigger flood. We use fittings matched to the tubing brand.

    Whole-House Water Softener Installation in Spearfish

    Hard water is the norm across much of South Dakota, and many groundwater sources carry dissolved minerals like calcium, magnesium, sulfate and iron. Softener sizing comes down to hardness times daily gallons, which tells us how many grains the resin has to remove between regenerations. A unit sized too small regenerates constantly and wastes salt. We set the drain line with an air gap. Programming follows, set to your hardness, and we run one test cycle.

    Sump Pump and Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Spearfish

    Basements and crawl spaces that take on water need a sump pump, and fixtures below the sewer line need an ejector. They are different pumps for different water, and one cannot stand in for the other. Sizing comes first. We match pump capacity to how fast the pit fills, put the pump on its own circuit and run the discharge with a check valve.

    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Spearfish, SD

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. Code requires CSST to be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Tankless Water Heater Installation in Spearfish, SD

    Navien and Rinnai tankless units hang on a wall and free up the floor space a tank used to take. Gas models usually need a bigger gas line and new stainless or PVC venting. Electric models need amperage. We size by gallons per minute and the temperature rise from your incoming water. Long, cold winters and hot summers put South Dakota plumbing through wide temperature swings that expand and contract pipes and fittings every year. Isolation valves go on every unit for descaling.

    Sump Pump Installation in Spearfish, SD

    A sump pump that fails during a storm usually failed months earlier: a stuck float, a seized impeller, a dead check valve or a cord unplugged for the shop vac. We dig the pit to the right depth, set a primary pump sized to the inflow and add a battery backup if you want one. Where the water exits matters most. We run the discharge line far enough from the foundation that the water does not cycle straight back into the pit.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Spearfish

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. A PEX repipe ends that cycle. The layout is a manifold with home runs for balanced pressure, or trunk and branch when the framing makes home runs a fight. We cut small, clean access holes and patch them, or hand off to your drywall contractor if you prefer.

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Soils in South Dakota range from glacial deposits in the east to clay rich ground farther west that swells when wet and shrinks when dry.
