    Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Spearfish, SD

    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. We figure out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe without opening the whole ceiling.

    Water Line Leak Detection in Spearfish, SD

    Supply leaks hide inside walls, above ceilings and under yards. Your water bill is usually the first warning. We read the meter, close fixture stops one at a time and check framing with a moisture meter, then confirm with acoustic gear on pressurized lines, which keeps the drywall cut small in your Spearfish home.

    Gas Line Leak Locating in Spearfish

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, and check every joint with soap and a detector. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe can signal a leak that has run for a long time. We coordinate with the gas utility.

    Our Leak Detection Equipment in Spearfish

    Our trucks carry acoustic correlators and ground microphones, thermal cameras, line tracers, moisture meters and tracer gas kits. Each tool has a job. Acoustic gear hears pressurized water escaping, thermal cameras show temperature differences from hot water lines and wet drywall, and tracer gas finds leaks that make no sound at all on plastic pipe.

    Gas Leak Detection in Spearfish

    A faint sulfur smell near the range or furnace needs a professional check. Leave the house if the odor is strong. Our licensed plumbers use a combustible gas detector and soap solution on fittings, appliance connectors and the meter riser, then pressure test the line to find a leak too small to smell, and we repair it to the fuel gas code before turning service back on.
