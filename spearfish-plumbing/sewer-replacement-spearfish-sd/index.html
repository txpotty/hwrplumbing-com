    Sewer Cleanout Installation in Spearfish, SD

    A lot of older houses have no outside cleanout. Without one, the plumber has to pull a toilet or go through the roof vent.     Cast Iron Sewer Replacement in Spearfish

    Cast iron sewer pipe lasts a long time, though not indefinitely. Over decades it scales up inside and the bottom wears through. We replace it with PVC, and where cast iron remains under the house we use a shielded no-hub coupling at the transition. Clean seal.

    Code-Compliant Sewer Replacement in Spearfish

    Code sets the minimum slope, the cleanout spacing and the pipe type. The inspector looks at fall, cleanouts and material before backfill. We set fall at a quarter inch per foot unless the run requires otherwise, place cleanouts at the house and at major direction changes, and use pipe and fittings listed for underground drainage. Paperwork stays on file.

    Open-Trench Sewer Replacement in Spearfish, SD

    Trenching lets us fix grade. Correcting slope is where it shines. When an old line has a sag that holds water and solids, bursting would just follow the same sag, so we dig and reset the pipe on a proper bed at a proper fall. Bellies disappear.

    Sewer Scope Inspections for Replacement in Spearfish

    Trenchless Pipe Bursting in Spearfish

    Bursting works best on clay, cast iron and Orangeburg. Roots cannot get into a joint that does not exist.
