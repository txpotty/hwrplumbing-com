    Water Heater Valve and Element Repair in Spearfish

    Well Pump Service in Spearfish, SD

    Submersible pumps sit deep in the casing, so diagnosis happens at the surface first. HWR Plumbing checks the pressure switch, the control box capacitor and the tank precharge, then measures amp draw on the motor. High amp draw usually means a worn motor or a seized impeller. Jet pumps are simpler, and a lost prime or a bad foot valve is the usual cause when one stops moving water.

    Under-Slab Line Repair in Spearfish, SD

    Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Spearfish
