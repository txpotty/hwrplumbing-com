    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Pipe sections run higher. Chemical cleaners can make things worse.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair fixes the pipe. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, so a drain that clogs repeatedly usually needs repair rather than more snaking. We check on video.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Vermillion may require a plumbing permit, depending on how much pipe is replaced and how the city defines the work. We pull it when one applies.

    Water coming up somewhere other than where you are draining. That is the big one. If the tub fills when the washer drains, or the toilet bubbles when the sink runs, the blockage is past the point where those fixtures join, usually in the main branch or the building drain. A camera confirms it.

    Small fixture drain repairs usually do not. Stack work typically does. Replacing drain stacks, relocating drains or adding new lines often needs a permit, depending on the city and how much of the system is being changed. The permit is on us if needed.

    Fixing the cause instead of the clog. Pitch is the big one. After we correct a sag, replace a rough fitting or add a missing vent, the line stays clear with normal use and a little care about grease. Older lines may still need yearly cleaning.
