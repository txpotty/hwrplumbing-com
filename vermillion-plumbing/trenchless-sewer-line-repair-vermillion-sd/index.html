    Cured-In-Place Pipe Lining in Vermillion, SD

    CIPP stands for cured-in-place pipe. We wet out a felt tube with epoxy resin, invert it into the cleaned line with air pressure, and hold it against the old pipe wall until the resin hardens. Cure time runs a few hours. The finished liner seals cracks and leaking joints in the original pipe.

    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Vermillion

    A bursting head is a steel cone, slightly wider than the old pipe, pulled through the line by a hydraulic unit or a winch cable. We use it when the old line is too broken or flattened to hold a liner.
