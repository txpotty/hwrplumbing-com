    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Vermillion

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small.     CSST Repair and Bonding in Vermillion, SD

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. Damaged runs get spliced with the manufacturer's fittings, and we confirm the bond on the way out.
