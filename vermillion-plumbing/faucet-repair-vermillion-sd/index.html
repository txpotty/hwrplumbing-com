    Everyday Faucet Repairs in Vermillion, SD

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Vermillion

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. A new faucet can usually go in on the same visit, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Vermillion, SD

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. A seats and springs kit fixes most of them. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Emergency Faucet Repair in Vermillion

    A faucet that will not shut off, or one with a cracked body spraying under the sink, needs the water off before anything else happens. Close the angle stops under the sink. If they will not turn, use the main shutoff for the house. We come fast for these.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Vermillion

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. Flushing usually clears it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Faucet Handle Repair and Replacement in Vermillion, SD

    Stripped handle splines, cracked handle bodies, worn adapters and loose set screws make a faucet hard to turn or impossible to shut tight. Handles are often sold separately. We match the handle to the stem or cartridge and the finish, and if the stem itself is stripped we replace it at the same time.

    Leaky Faucet Repair in Vermillion

    Most drips come from the part that seals the water inside the faucet, which is a rubber washer on older two-handle faucets and a cartridge or ceramic disc on newer ones. We figure out which type you have first. It matters. With the right part on the truck, most drips are fixed in one visit.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Vermillion, SD

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. The pipe stub gets measured so we bring the right spout, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Vermillion

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.
