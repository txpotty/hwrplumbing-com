    Septic Lid and Riser Repair in Vermillion

    Old concrete lids crack, and a cracked lid over a 1,000-gallon tank is a real danger to kids and pets. Bad lids get replaced, and risers bring the opening up to grade. Future pumping gets cheaper. Every lid we set is screwed down or locked so a child cannot lift it.

    Septic Tee Repair in Vermillion

    Sanitary tees on the inlet and outlet crack or pop loose, sometimes from a careless pump-out hose. Without the outlet tee, the floating scum layer drifts into the laterals and plugs the gravel. We glue in a new Schedule 40 tee. An effluent filter goes on if the outlet has room.

    Inlet and Outlet Baffle Replacement in Vermillion, SD

    Baffles hold back the scum layer on top and the sludge on the bottom so only clear effluent leaves the tank. Concrete ones corrode from the acid that sewer gas forms and eventually fall off into the tank. With no outlet baffle, sludge flows right out to the leach lines. We replace them with PVC sanitary tees. Usually a short job.

    Drain Field Line Jetting in Vermillion

    Distribution laterals clog from the inside with solids and slime, especially after years without pumping. We jet each lateral from the distribution box or a cleanout at the far end, and what washes back shows how plugged it was. Roots get cut. If the soil itself has sealed, jetting will not fix it, and we say so.

    Emergency Septic Overflow in Vermillion

    Raw sewage coming up through a tub drain carries bacteria you do not want on your skin or your floors. Keep kids and pets away from it and stop all water use. We find the blockage and get flow moving, sometimes with a jetter and sometimes with a pump truck. Cleanup guidance comes with it.

    Septic Alarm Troubleshooting in Vermillion

    A septic alarm sounding is the system's way of saying the water is too high in the pump chamber or the aerobic unit. Hit the mute switch and go easy on the water. We check whether the pump runs and whether a float is hung up on its cord. A tripped breaker is the cheapest answer.
