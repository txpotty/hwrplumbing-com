    Once a year is the minimum. Each yearly visit covers the motor, the impeller, the float and the discharge line. After that, we fill the basin and time the drawdown, since a slow pump-out is the first hint of a tired impeller or a restricted line.

    Check Valve and Discharge Pipe Repair in Sturgis, SD

    A bad check valve can nearly double how often the pump starts. Each start heats the motor windings. We swap worn valves for quiet, spring-loaded models and inspect the discharge pipe for cracks and loose couplings all the way to the exit point. The water should exit well away from the slab or foundation so it does not soak back toward the pit.

    Submersible Sump Pump Service in Sturgis

    Submersible pumps sit at the bottom of the pit, which keeps them quiet but hides the problems. We lift the unit out to look at the seals and the cord. A cracked cord usually means a new pump. The intake screen gets cleaned, and we look over the housing for corrosion, since pumps sitting in standing water for years rust from the bottom up.

    Home Sump Pump Maintenance in Sturgis

    A typical house pit is about 18 inches across and two feet deep, and most of what goes wrong happens inside it. We pull the pump, clear the intake screen and scoop out the silt.     Pit Cleaning and Liner Checks in Sturgis

    Long dry stretches let dust, sand, leaves and gravel settle at the bottom of the basin. Sooner or later the pump sucks it into the screen and locks up the impeller. Our plumber pumps the basin down, rinses the screen and inspects the liner for cracks that let dirt seep in through the walls. Takes about twenty minutes.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Sturgis

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. A backup pump keeps water moving when the main pump loses power or quits. We load test the battery, check the charger and the alarm, and run the backup by itself with the main pump unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Battery Backup System Testing in Sturgis, SD

    Storms knock out power when the pit fills fastest. A backup pump exists for exactly that hour. We check the battery date, clean the terminals and confirm the charger is actually charging, since a dead charger quietly drains the battery to nothing over a few months.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Sturgis, SD

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. Our plumber load tests the battery instead of just reading its voltage. Next, we cut power to the main pump and watch the backup clear the basin by itself. Alarms get tested too.
