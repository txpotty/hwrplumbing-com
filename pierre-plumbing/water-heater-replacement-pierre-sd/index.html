    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Pierre

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. We price both options.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Pierre

    Gas Water Heater Replacement in Pierre, SD

    Gas water heaters recover faster than electric, and in Pierre, SD homes they fail most often from a corroded tank, a bad gas valve or a burner clogged with rust flakes. We shut off the gas, disconnect and cap the line, drain the old tank and set the new one, then install a new flex connector, a sediment trap and a vent connector sloped up toward the flue. Then we leak test.
