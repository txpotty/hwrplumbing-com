    Pipe Bursting and Pulling in Pierre, SD

    Bursting fits old cast iron or galvanized lines that will shatter under the head. Pulling suits softer pipe like poly or copper. Either way the new pipe follows the old path, which matters when the route runs under a patio or a neighbor's fence, leaving you with two small holes instead of a long trench. Rock can stop it.

    Type L vs. Type K Copper in Pierre, SD

    The thickest wall belongs to Type K, the usual choice underground. Type L is thinner and mostly used inside the house. Some codes allow L for burial, but we stick with K for service lines because a thicker wall buys more years against corrosive soil and stray rocks. Same fittings, different pipe.

    Burst Main Line Response in Pierre, SD

    Burst lines do damage fast. We get the water off, dig to the break, and decide on site whether a clamp or a short section of new pipe will hold until a full replacement can be scheduled, or whether the line is too far gone to bother patching. You will not be without water overnight if we can help it.

    PEX Service Lines for Long Runs in Pierre

    PEX comes in coils up to several hundred feet. That makes it the easy pick when the meter sits out at the road and the house is set far back, because one coil covers the whole run without a joint. Fewer joints underground means fewer places a leak can start, and it is the joints that usually fail first.

    Water Line Upgrades and Code Compliance in Pierre, SD

    A lot of older houses were fed with a 1/2-inch or 3/4-inch line that was fine for one bathroom. Add a second bath and a dishwasher and it falls short. When we replace the line we size it to your fixture count, which often means going to 1-inch, and bring the shutoff, depth and backflow pieces up to what code asks for today. Better pressure upstairs.

    Main Water Line Repair vs. Replacement in Pierre

    One leak on a twenty-year-old copper line? We will usually just fix that spot. Three leaks on a galvanized line from the 1950s is different, because the pipe is thin everywhere and the next leak is already forming, so replacing the whole run is cheaper over five years. We tell you which camp you are in and show you the pipe we cut out.
