    Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Pierre, SD

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. Pumping alone leaves that crust in place. Jetting the empty tank and its inlet line strips it back to bare walls and gets back the capacity that crust was taking. Plan on about an extra hour.

    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Pierre, SD

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. Busy kitchens need them cleaned every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened. Most odor from these units comes from a bad gasket.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Pierre

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. Each compartment gets pumped separately. A hard grease cap gets broken up with a pressure wand before pumping, otherwise it plugs the suction hose and half of it stays in the tank. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.

    Odor Control and Trap Inspections in Pierre

    A trap that stinks between pump-outs usually has a flattened lid gasket or a vent problem. Both get checked every visit. Gaskets are cheap to swap, but a broken vent can push sewer gas into the kitchen and slow the drain too, so it is worth chasing. Most smells get fixed that visit.

    Outdoor Grease Interceptor Pumping in Pierre, SD

    In-ground interceptors need a vacuum truck and usually a pressure wand to break up the cap. Every compartment gets opened and pumped to the bottom. With the tank empty, we inspect the tees, baffle wall, risers and lid seals, since a cracked riser lets dirt and groundwater into the tank. Lids go back on tight.

    Emergency Grease Trap Cleaning in Pierre, SD

    Sometimes the trap is fine and the problem is downstream. We find that out fast with a camera. A trap that is half empty while floor sinks keep backing up points to a clog in the lateral toward the main, which needs jetting, not another pump-out. We can jet on the same visit.

We also check the gasket and the flow control.
