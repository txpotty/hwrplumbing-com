    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Brookings, SD

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. If scale comes back or pressure drops, the media or the prefilter is usually the problem. HWR Plumbing repairs these units in Brookings, SD. Media lasts a few years.

    Salt Usage and Settings Repair in Brookings

    Too much salt or water use usually means settings that do not match your hardness or household. An old timer head regenerates by the calendar, used or not. After retesting hardness, we reprogram the valve or swap in a metered head that regenerates only when needed. Salt bills drop.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Brookings

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold.
