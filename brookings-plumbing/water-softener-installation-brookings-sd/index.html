    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Brookings

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. It complements a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Softener Installation in Brookings

    Most Brookings, SD softener installs land between $1,500 and $4,000. That typically covers the unit itself and the labor to plumb it in, program it and test it, with any loop or drain work listed separately. Bigger homes cost more. We give written pricing up front.

    Salt-based softeners add a small amount of sodium. Plenty of homeowners add an RO system at the kitchen sink for drinking and cooking.

    White, chalky scale on faucets and dishes is the most common sign. Dry skin, weak soap lather and dull laundry are other clues. You likely have hard water.

    Some homeowners try it, but we recommend a plumber. Cutting into the main, setting the bypass, running the drain to an air gap and programming the valve all have to be right, or you get leaks and poor softening. Warranties often require pro installs.

    Ten to fifteen years is typical for a quality softener. Hardness, maintenance and build quality matter, and fresh resin can add years. Service helps.
