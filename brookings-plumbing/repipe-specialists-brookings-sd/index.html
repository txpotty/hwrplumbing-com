    What Our Repipe Crews Do in Brookings, SD

    Commercial Repiping in Brookings, SD

    Commercial buildings in Brookings, SD often have larger pipe, risers that serve several floors and tenants who cannot lose water for a full day. Our crew breaks commercial repipes into zones, working one riser or suite at a time while the rest of the building keeps water. Night work is possible.     Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Brookings

    Copper is the traditional choice, and Type L copper has a thicker wall than the Type M that went into many older homes. Type L tolerates UV, stays rigid and has decades of field history. It costs more. Our plumbers solder joints with lead-free solder or use press fittings, support the pipe at the spacing the code calls for and pressure test the system before any wall is closed.

    PEX Pipe Replacement in Brookings, SD

    PEX tubing resists freezing damage, corrosion and scale better than most metal pipe. It is quieter too. Because PEX flexes, it absorbs some of the shock that causes water hammer, and long runs with few fittings mean fewer places for a leak to start inside a wall. We keep PEX out of sunlight and away from water heater flues.

    PEX Repiping in Brookings, SD

    PEX is flexible plastic tubing that can run long distances without a fitting, which means fewer joints hidden in your walls. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. It resists scale and chlorine, handles a freeze better than copper and costs less to install, which is why most of our repipes use it. Our PEX-A installs use expansion fittings that do not shrink the flow path. That keeps pressure up at the far fixtures.

    Repipe Costs Versus Leak Repairs in Brookings, SD

    A whole-home PEX repipe for an average house often runs $5,000 to $12,000, with copper costing more. A single slab leak repair can run $1,000 to $4,000 once access and restoration are included. Do the math. By the second or third leak, a repipe usually pays for itself, and we put both options side by side so you can decide.

    PEX-A Whole-Home Repiping in Brookings

    PEX-A is flexible, resists scale and handles thermal expansion well, which suits hard water and long attic runs. Our crews use Uponor or similar PEX-A, where the pipe end gets expanded and shrinks back over the fitting for a full-diameter connection. Fewer joints, fewer leaks. Most homes are repiped with PEX-A in two to three days.
