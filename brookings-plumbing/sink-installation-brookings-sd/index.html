    How We Set New Sinks in Brookings, SD

    Vessel Sink Installation in Brookings, SD

    Vessel sinks sit on top of the counter. That raises the rim several inches, so we either pick a taller vessel faucet or mount a wall faucet, and we use a vessel drain that matches the bowl's overflow or lack of one. Bowls without an overflow need an unslotted drain. Getting that wrong leaves water under the bowl.

    Kitchen Sink Replacement in Brookings

    Pulling the old kitchen sink is half the job. Strainers are often fused on, the disposal has to come off, and the dishwasher hose needs a fresh high loop. After that, the new bowl goes in. We reuse the disposal if it still runs well.

    Bathroom Sink Installation in Brookings, SD

    Bathroom sinks are lighter but fussier. Faucet holes must match the faucet, single hole, 4 inch centerset or 8 inch widespread, and the overflow must suit the drain. The spec sheet gets read first. Mismatches get caught before the counter is cut.

    Bathroom Vanity Sink Installation in Brookings, SD

    Vanity tops with integrated bowls are the easiest bath install. The top sits on the cabinet in a bead of silicone, the faucet and pop-up go on first, and the trap arm usually needs a small adjustment to meet the new tailpiece. Backsplash comes last. We caulk it to the wall.

    Double Vanity Sink Installation in Brookings

    A second bowl means a second drain and two more supplies. We tie both drains into the wall with either two separate traps or a continuous waste to one trap, depending on the spacing, and add a new pair of stops for the second faucet. Vent sizing gets checked. Both bowls should drain without gurgling.

    Undermount Sink Installation in Brookings

    Undermount sinks hang from the countertop. We set the bowl with the clips or anchors the stone shop provides, run a continuous bead of 100 percent silicone around the rim, and leave it braced while the adhesive cures overnight. Adhesive by itself will not hold it. Plumbing goes on the next day.

    Pedestal Sink Installation in Brookings, SD

    Pedestal sinks hide almost nothing. The supply stops and the trap sit right behind the column, so we center the drain stub, use chrome or polished brass trap parts, and set the stops tight to the wall where the pedestal can hide them. Blocking in the wall is a must. The basin hangs from it, not the pedestal.

    Sink Installation Cost Factors in Brookings, SD

    Labor is the main cost driver. Replacing old galvanized stubs or adding a second sink adds hours, while a same-size drop-in swap with good shutoffs is the cheapest job we do on any given week. The sink price is separate. You can buy it or we can.
