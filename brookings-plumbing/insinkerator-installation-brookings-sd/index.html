    Standard InSinkErator Installation in Brookings

    A standard install replaces the unit under an existing sink. The old disposal comes down, the flange gets scraped clean and a new one goes in on fresh putty. Then the mount ring goes on. We hang the unit, turn the lower ring until it locks and connect the discharge tube to the trap with a new slip-joint washer so nothing weeps. We connect power through a cord kit or the old hardwire and confirm the ground first.

    Dishwasher Connection and Air Gap Setup in Brookings

    Nearly every InSinkErator has a dishwasher inlet with a factory knockout plug inside. The plug gets knocked loose and pulled out of the grind chamber. Then the dishwasher hose goes on with a proper clamp, routed through an air gap or up into a high loop under the counter so sink water cannot siphon back into the dishwasher tub. Both appliances get run together.

    Disposal Installs for New Sinks in Brookings, SD

    A new sink changes the drain math. Undermount and farmhouse bowls are often deeper than the old drop-in. A deeper bowl pushes the disposal lower, and if the trap arm in the wall was set for a shallow sink, the discharge can end up below it, so we sometimes lower the wall stub. We plan that during the remodel, not after.

    Septic-Friendly Disposal Installs in Brookings, SD

    Homes on septic can run a disposal, but food solids add load to the tank. Rural service calls here can mean long distances between homes, so scheduling well pump and septic checks before winter is a smart move. The Evolution Septic Assist injects an enzyme solution into the grind chamber each time it runs, which helps break down food waste before it reaches the tank. We mount the cartridge and show you how to swap it. Scrape plates first.
