    PEX-A, PEX-B, and PEX-C Systems in Brookings, SD

    There are three grades of PEX, named for how the plastic is cross-linked at the factory. PEX-A is the most flexible and joins with expansion rings, PEX-B is stiffer and joins with crimp rings or cinch clamps, and PEX-C sits closer to B in feel. All three are code-listed for drinking water. Pipe, fittings and tools come from the same maker on our jobs, which keeps the warranty valid.

    New Construction PEX Plumbing in Brookings, SD

    On a new build, HWR Plumbing runs PEX supply lines while the framing is still open in Brookings, SD. It keeps the schedule moving.

    Emergency PEX Pipe Repair & Installation in Brookings, SD

    PEX rarely fails on its own. When it does, the cause is usually a nail or screw driven through it, a rodent chewing on an attic run, or a fitting that was never crimped all the way. Our trucks stock half-inch to one-inch pipe and fittings. Most emergency PEX repairs in Brookings, SD are a cut-and-splice job that takes under an hour once the drywall is open.

    PEX-B Crimp and Clamp Systems in Brookings

    PEX-B is stiffer, costs less per foot, and joins with copper crimp rings or stainless cinch clamps over brass or poly-alloy fittings. A crimp joint is only as good as the tool, so we check every ring with a go/no-go gauge, and a ring that does not pass gets cut out and redone. It is a five-second check. Skipping it causes most crimp leaks.

    PEX-A vs. PEX-B Installation in Brookings

    Homeowners ask us which one is better, and it really depends on the house. PEX-A bends tighter, recovers from a kink with a heat gun, and its expansion fittings flow more water, while PEX-B costs less and its crimp joints are just as reliable when they are gauged properly. Both last decades. In a cramped attic, we usually pick PEX-A.

    Full Home PEX Repiping in Brookings
