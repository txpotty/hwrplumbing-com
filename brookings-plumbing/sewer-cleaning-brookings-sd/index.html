    Clogged Main Sewer Line Solutions in Brookings, SD

    Several drains backing up at once is a main line problem. One pipe, many fixtures. We clear it with a sectional cable or a jetter, then camera the line to see whether the cause was roots, debris or a structural problem like a broken joint or an offset section.

    Residential Sewer Cleaning in Brookings

    Household sewers clog slowly. It takes months of buildup to stop a main. Debris catches at a rough joint or a sag, and every load of laundry adds to it until the line closes, and we clear it with a sectional cable or a small jetter sized for the pipe.

    Hydro Jetting for Sewer Lines in Brookings

    Jetting cleans the whole pipe wall. A cable only makes a path. Water at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI strips grease, scale and fine roots off the inside of the line and flushes it all toward the street, which leaves the pipe close to its original diameter.

    Tree Root Removal in Brookings, SD Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.
