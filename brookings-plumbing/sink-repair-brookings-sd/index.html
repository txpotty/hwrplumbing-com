    Outdoor and Utility Sink Repair in Brookings

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a far larger flood. UV light cracks plastic traps and supply lines left outdoors, and dirt from the yard clogs the drain. Hose bib leaks are part of the same call. A frost-free sillcock is worth it on exposed walls.

    After-Hours Sink Leak Repair in Brookings, SD

    Leaks do not keep office hours. A burst supply line can dump gallons a minute, and we answer at night and on weekends with a stocked truck. While you wait, shut off the valves under the sink. If those will not hold, use the main.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Brookings, SD

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We run a camera or a longer cable into the wall arm and look for a sagging line or buildup where the sink ties into the tub drain. Sometimes the vent is blocked. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Sink Parts Replacement in Brookings, SD

    Most sinks do not need replacing. Swapping a tailpiece or pop-up costs far less than a new sink, and you keep the bowl and faucet you already have. We match finish where we can. Discontinued faucets sometimes force a full faucet swap.

    Office and Restaurant Sink Repair in Brookings

    Office breakroom sinks get abused in ways a home sink never sees. Coffee grounds and paper towels go down the drain all day, and the trap behind the cabinet door may not get looked at until it overflows onto the carpet. We clear it and add a strainer. For restaurants, we keep three-bowl sinks running between the lunch and dinner rush.

    Slow or Clogged Sink Drain Repair in Brookings, SD

    Slow drains almost always clog at the trap or the first few feet past the wall. In kitchens it is cold grease and food, and in bathrooms it is hair knotted on the stopper and glued with soap scum. A hand auger does most of the work. Hydro-jetting is for main lines, not sink arms.

    Loose or Wobbly Sink Repair in Brookings

    A sink that moves when you lean on it is pulling on the drain. Undermount sinks hang on clips glued under the counter, and pedestal sinks hang on a bracket that can pull loose from drywall. We reset the clips and reseal the rim. Pedestals get blocking behind the wall when needed.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Brookings, SD

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. We pull the drain and reseal it. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
