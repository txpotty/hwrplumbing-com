    Locating and Fixing Foundation Leaks in Brookings, SD

    Water Line Reroute in Brookings

    A reroute leaves the old pipe under the slab and runs new line through the attic or walls. No concrete gets broken. We usually run PEX because it bends around framing and does not corrode, and the drywall openings are cut small, often just a few squares at fixture walls, so patching goes fast.

    Drain Line Leak Under Foundation in Brookings, SD

    Drain leaks under a slab are harder to find. Drains carry no pressure. A hydrostatic test shows whether the line holds water, and a camera run shows us the cracked fitting, offset joint or roots causing it. Fixes include tunneling, slab cuts or a pipe liner, and we explain each one.

    Crawl Space Foundation Leak Repair in Brookings, SD

    Crawl spaces hide leaks from supply lines, drains and AC condensate lines. Look for wet insulation. Water sitting under the house rots sill plates and floor joists and feeds mold on the subfloor, so we find the source, fix the pipe and check whether the vapor barrier needs replacing. A sump pump may make sense in a Brookings, SD crawl space that floods from outside.

    Foundation Leak Repair Cost Comparison in Brookings, SD

    Direct repairs usually cost least up front. Flooring adds to it. A reroute costs somewhat more but avoids concrete work and future leaks in the abandoned pipe, and a repipe fits homes that have already leaked more than once. We line up the full cost of each, flooring and drywall included.

    Basement Wall Sealing and Waterproofing in Brookings, SD

    Basement walls in Brookings, SD leak through cracks, tie holes and the joint where the wall meets the floor. Injection fixes cracks. We inject polyurethane or epoxy into single cracks, and for walls that weep along the bottom, we put in an interior drain tile and sump so water has somewhere to go besides your floor.

    Slab Leaks Under Commercial Buildings in Brookings

    Commercial slabs carry more plumbing and more foot traffic than a house. They leak for the same reasons. Leaks get located and repaired around your hours, and reroute work can happen overnight so customers never notice. Property owners and insurers receive a written report with photos.
