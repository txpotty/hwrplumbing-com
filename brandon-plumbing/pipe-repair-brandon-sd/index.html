    PEX and CPVC Pipe Repair in Brandon, SD

    Plastic supply pipe like PEX and CPVC is common in homes built over the last few decades. Each fails its own way.     Pinhole Leak Repair in Brandon

    Pinhole leaks form when the copper wall thins from the inside. Water quality is often behind it.
