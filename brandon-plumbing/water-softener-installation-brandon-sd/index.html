    Softener and Filter Installs in Brandon, SD

    Reverse Osmosis Drinking Water Systems in Brandon

    Reverse osmosis pushes water through a fine membrane that removes most dissolved solids, including the sodium a softener adds. Our RO installs go under the sink with their own faucet, and we can tie in the fridge or ice maker too. Pairs well with a softener. We can handle filter changes.

    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Brandon, SD

    Salt-based softeners use ion exchange, swapping calcium and magnesium for sodium on a bed of resin beads. HWR Plumbing installs these with a properly sized resin tank and brine tank. We program regeneration by metered water use so the system only recharges when the resin is actually spent, which saves a lot of salt. They work best on very hard water.

    Whole-House Water Softener Systems in Brandon

    On a whole-house system, the softener goes on the main line after the shutoff and before the water heater. Every whole-house install gets a bypass valve, and we add a pressure reducing valve if incoming pressure is too high. Every tap gets soft water. Outdoor taps can be left off.

    High-Efficiency Water Softener Installation in Brandon

    High-efficiency softeners use upflow brining and metered valves to cut salt and water use. Compared with an older timer-based unit, a modern metered softener can use roughly half the salt and noticeably less regeneration water each month. The savings add up. We install several models.

    Ecodyne Water Softener Installation in Brandon

    Ecodyne softeners are sold at many home stores, and we install them for homeowners who already bought one. Install follows the manufacturer manual for bypass, drain and overflow, then we set hardness and regeneration. We install store-bought units. Programming is part of the job.

    Whole-Home Water Softener Installation in Brandon

    Whole-home softeners treat every gallon that enters the house, right after the main shutoff. That protects every faucet, shower, water heater and appliance downstream, which is where hard water does most of its damage over the years. Sizing comes first. Your test result and water use decide the tank size.

    Residential Water Softener Installation in Brandon

    Hard water costs homeowners money in shorter appliance life and extra cleaning. We test the water, ask about daily use and choose a capacity that lets the system regenerate roughly once a week. That keeps salt use reasonable. Every detail is handled on one visit.

    Fleck Water Softener Installation in Brandon, SD

    Fleck valves, especially the 5600SXT and 7000SXT, are on a lot of the softeners we install. They are simple to program, easy to rebuild and parts are stocked almost everywhere, which matters when a seal fails ten years in. Settings match your test. We confirm the setup before leaving.
