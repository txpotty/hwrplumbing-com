    Root Intrusion Repair in Brandon, SD

    Cutting roots treats the symptom. The joint is the real problem. After clearing the line, we look at each rooted joint on camera and decide between a short sectional liner, a full liner or cutting in new pipe, depending on how many joints are affected and how far apart they sit. One or two joints is a small job. Ten or twelve usually justifies lining the run.

    Restaurant and Retail Sewer Repair in Brandon

    Restaurants, retail centers and multifamily buildings load a lateral far beyond a single house. Grease is the usual culprit.     Sewer Repair Cost Comparison in Brandon, SD

    A spot repair is the cheapest fix when the rest of the line is sound. Lining costs more per foot.     Sewer Line Leak Detection and Repair in Brandon, SD

    Sewer smell outside the house points to a leak. So does an extra-green strip of lawn.     Video Camera Sewer Inspection in Brandon

    A self-leveling camera goes in at the cleanout or through a pulled toilet. The full length gets recorded. Cracks, root intrusion, offset joints and standing water all show up on the screen, and the sonde in the camera head lets us mark the exact spot and depth on the ground above, which keeps the dig to one small pit.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Brandon, SD

    Relining builds a new pipe inside the old one. The old pipe gets jetted clean before anything else. A flexible liner saturated with epoxy is pulled or inverted into place, inflated against the old wall and left to cure, which seals cracks and root entry points in a single continuous tube. The finished pipe loses a little diameter. For most houses the flow difference is negligible.
