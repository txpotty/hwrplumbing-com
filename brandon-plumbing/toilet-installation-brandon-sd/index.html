    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Brandon, SD

    New tile over old vinyl can raise a bathroom floor by half an inch or more. Suddenly the old flange is below the floor.     Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Brandon

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill.     Smart Toilet Installation in Brandon, SD

    Smart toilets with heated seats and built-in bidets need two things the old toilet did not: power and a dedicated water connection. Most need a GFCI outlet close enough for the cord. We run the water side and mount the unit, and if a new outlet is needed we bring in a licensed electrician rather than splicing anything ourselves. Then we test every button.

    Two-Piece Toilet Installation in Brandon

    Two-piece toilets come with the tank and bowl separate, joined by a rubber gasket and two or three bolts. We assemble them on the floor before setting. Even tightening, alternating sides, matters more than force, since uneven pressure either cracks the china or leaves one bolt weeping. It should not wobble.

    Urgent Toilet Replacement in Brandon

    A cracked tank or a bowl leaking at the base cannot wait for next week, especially in a house with one bathroom. We keep common two-piece toilets on the truck.     New Construction Toilet Installation in Brandon

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. An early set means the tile goes around the flange, leaving it low, and that calls for an extender ring later. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Brandon, SD

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

PVC and ABS flanges crack when someone overtightens the bolts, and a repair ring screwed over the top is often all it takes to restore a solid anchor. Seal goes on last.
