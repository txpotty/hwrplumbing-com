    Foundation Crack Repair in Brandon

    Crack injection takes about two hours per crack.     Sump Pump Installation and Repair in Brandon, SD

    A sump pump is the part of an interior system that actually moves the water. We set a lidded basin below slab level, install a cast iron pump sized to the water coming in, and add a battery backup or water-powered backup for the night the power goes out during a storm. Check valves matter. A missing check valve lets the discharge line drain back into the pit and the pump short-cycles. The discharge line gets routed at least 10 feet from the house.

    Foundation Crack Repair & Sealing in Brandon

    Hairline cracks in poured concrete walls leak more water than their size suggests. Horizontal cracks are different. A horizontal crack often signals soil pressure pushing the wall in, so we check for movement with a level before we seal anything. Vertical cracks are usually a one-visit job.
