    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Brandon, SD

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. Expect more motor noise than a submersible. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump System Installation in Brandon, SD

    Two pumps on one riser cover both kinds of failure, a dead motor and a dead outlet. The primary runs on house power and handles the everyday inflow, while the battery unit waits above it on a separate float. Finished basements are the usual reason. We install them in homes where a flood would ruin flooring and drywall.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Brandon

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. They can go on a new pump or one that is already working.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Brandon, SD

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. Most run five to seven hours on a full charge. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Commercial Sump Pump Solutions in Brandon

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and loading dock drains need pumps rated for more volume. We install duplex systems with alternating pumps. Downtime costs money. We plan the install around your business hours.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Brandon, SD

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Exterior Drainage and Yard Sump Pumps in Brandon

    Sunken patios, below-grade driveways and deep window wells collect water with nowhere to go. For those areas we set an exterior basin with a submersible pump and run the discharge to a safe point, often a pop-up emitter in the yard. Slopes toward the house are the usual cause.
