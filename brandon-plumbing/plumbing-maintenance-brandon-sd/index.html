    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Records support warranty claims.

    Repipe Assessments in Brandon, SD

    Water Softener Service in Brandon

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. Salt level, brine tank, injector and a test regeneration all get checked. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Sump Pump Maintenance in Brandon, SD

    Spring snowmelt and heavy rain can raise rivers and groundwater quickly in South Dakota, which often shows up as water in basements and overwhelmed sump pumps.     Hot Water Tank Service in Brandon, SD

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Scale on the tank floor wastes energy and shortens tank life.
