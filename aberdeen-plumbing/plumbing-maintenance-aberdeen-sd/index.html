    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Aberdeen

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. The inlet filter, venting and gas line get checked too.

    Softener Cleaning and Testing in Aberdeen

    Hard water scale builds in pipes and appliances fast. Brine, injector, resin and regeneration all get checked, and we test the water at a tap. Fouled resin can be cleaned. Worn resin gets swapped.

    Hot Water Tank Service in Aberdeen, SD

    Gas and electric tanks need different checks. Gas tanks get a burner, flue and leak check, and electric tanks get element and thermostat tests. Both get flushed. Mineral buildup on the bottom wastes fuel and can lead to early tank failure.

    Getting Ready for the Hard Months in Aberdeen, SD

    Weak spots show up in bad weather. Frozen pipes are a real and regular risk in South Dakota, with deep frost each winter that can reach water service lines buried at shallow depth. During maintenance we look for exposed pipes in garages and crawl spaces, hose bibs without vacuum breakers and outdoor lines that need draining, and we fix those before the season that tests them. Pipe sleeves cost little. Water damage costs plenty.
