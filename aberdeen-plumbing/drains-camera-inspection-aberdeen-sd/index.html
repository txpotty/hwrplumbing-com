    Identifying Specific Pipe Damage in Aberdeen

    Each kind of damage looks different on camera. A crack is a thin dark seam. Offsets show as a lip where one pipe section has dropped below the next, bellies show up as standing water that the camera head dips into, and corrosion on cast iron looks like rough, flaking scale along the bottom of the pipe. The type of damage decides the fix.
