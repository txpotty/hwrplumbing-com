    Sump Pump Installs in Aberdeen, SD

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Aberdeen, SD

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Aberdeen

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. Install time is shorter too. They suit homes that want backup protection but do not want to enlarge the pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Aberdeen

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi monitors go further and send a phone alert when the pump runs too long, the power drops or the water climbs past a set level. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Liberty Pumps Installation in Aberdeen, SD

    Liberty builds quiet, well-made submersible pumps with good switch options. We install their 250 and 280 series often. Parts are easy to find. We drill a 3/16 inch weep hole in the discharge to stop air lock.

    New Sump Pit Installation in Aberdeen

    No pit yet? We cut one at the low corner. Washed gravel goes around the basin so water can reach it. Then we patch the slab. The pump, lid and discharge go in last.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Installation in Aberdeen, SD

    Zoeller has built cast iron sump pumps for decades, and parts are easy to get. The M53 and the M98 are the two we set most, depending on lift and inflow, and both come with mechanical float switches that hold up for years. They make matching backup units. We help you pick the model that fits the pit.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Aberdeen, SD

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Batteries need replacing every few years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Aberdeen

    A primary sump pump is the one doing the daily work, kicking on every time the float rises in the pit after rain or snowmelt. We size it from pit diameter and vertical lift. Most homes need 1/3 or 1/2 HP. A 1-1/2 inch discharge line handles the flow from either size without straining the motor.
