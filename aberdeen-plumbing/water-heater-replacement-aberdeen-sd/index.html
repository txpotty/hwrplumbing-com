    Bradford White Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen

    Electric Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen

    Electric water heaters in Aberdeen usually fail from a burned-out heating element, a bad thermostat or a tank that has finally rusted through at the bottom. A bad element can be replaced. A leaking tank cannot, so if water is coming from the tank body itself, we replace the heater and match the voltage, wattage and breaker size to what your panel already supplies. Most swaps take a few hours.

    Attic Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen

    Housing in South Dakota mixes older farmhouses and town homes with newer subdivisions, so plumbing can range from cast iron and galvanized to modern PEX. Attic water heaters are harder to swap than ones in a garage or closet. Getting a 50-gallon tank up a pull-down ladder or through a narrow scuttle takes two people and care, and code calls for a drain pan with a line to the outside or a safe drain, a service platform and a light near the unit. We bring the help.

    Hybrid Water Heater Installation in Aberdeen, SD

    Hybrid heat pump water heaters pull heat from the air around them, using a fraction of the electricity a standard electric tank uses. They need room and airflow, about 700 cubic feet or a louvered door, plus a condensate drain, and they do best in a garage or basement that stays above freezing. They also cool the room.

    Commercial Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen

    Businesses in Aberdeen put far more hours on a heater than a house, so commercial replacements need more capacity and faster recovery. Restaurants, salons, laundromats and offices each have a different demand curve, and we size the new unit by peak gallons per hour and the temperature health code requires, which can mean a larger tank, a booster or a rack of tankless units. Downtime costs money.

    Upsizing Your Water Heater in Aberdeen

    Running out of hot water usually means the house outgrew the tank. A larger tank or a model with a higher first-hour rating can keep up with more showers back to back, and in some homes a 50-gallon high-recovery gas unit does the job of a 75-gallon standard tank without taking up any more floor space. Bigger is not always needed.

    Rheem and Ruud Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen, SD

    Ruud and Rheem come out of the same company and share many parts. Our technicians install both, and since the parts overlap, a Ruud thermostat or element is easy to source when a repair makes more sense than a full replacement on a newer tank. We will say when repair works.

    Tankless Water Heater Upgrades in Aberdeen

    Going tankless when the old tank fails is a popular move, but it is a bigger job than a tank swap. Since tankless can pull four times the gas of a tank, the meter line usually gets bigger, and the old vent is swapped for stainless or PVC. You see both numbers.
