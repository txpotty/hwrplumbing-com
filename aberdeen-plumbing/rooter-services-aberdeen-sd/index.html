    Hydro-Jetting Services in Aberdeen, SD

    Jetting puts water through a nozzle at 3,000 to 4,000 psi and scrubs the pipe wall clean. Grease, scale and fine roots that a cable rides over come off with the water. Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a water softener is a common upgrade for homeowners. We camera the line first. Pressure that high can damage cracked clay or thin cast iron, so we jet only after we have looked at the pipe.
