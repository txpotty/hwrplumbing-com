    Sump Pump Installation & Repair in Aberdeen

    Frequently Asked Questions About Basement Leak Repair in Aberdeen

    Basement plumbing leak repairs in Aberdeen typically cost $200 to $1,500, depending on the pipe and how hard it is to reach. Pipe on an open ceiling is the cheap case. Pipe behind finished drywall or under the slab adds the cost of opening and patching, and a cast iron stack replacement can run higher than the range above if it goes through more than one floor.

    Simple pipe repairs and crack injection usually do not need a permit. Bigger jobs often do. Stack replacement, new sumps and backwater valves often need one, and we handle it.

    Only some repairs need excavation. Outside membranes and new footing drains require digging to the footing. Inside repairs avoid the yard, which helps when a patio or driveway sits against the wall.

    Crack repairs use two-part polyurethane or epoxy injection resin, depending on whether the crack is actively wet. Polyurethane handles wet cracks. Our interior drains use rigid perforated pipe, which keeps its slope and can be jetted later.

    For sump pumps we mostly install Zoeller and Liberty, with Wayne for some battery backups. Cast iron housings outlast plastic ones. Injection resins and drain membranes come from commercial suppliers, matched so the parts fit.

    It depends on the scope. A small pipe or fitting repair generally does not. Bigger work like a new stack or a backwater valve may need one, and we pull it.

    Leak repair stops a specific source, like a cracked pipe or a leaking wall crack. Waterproofing covers the whole wall. It handles groundwater all around the foundation and makes sense when water comes in at many points.

    Check the base of the walls after a heavy rain. Damp bands low on the wall and water at the cove joint are typical. With everything turned off, a moving water meter means a plumbing leak. Note where the water shows.
