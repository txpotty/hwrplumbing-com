    Why Choose HWR Plumbing for Grease Trap Repair in Aberdeen, SD?

    Missing outlet tees are the single most common repair we make. Pumpers sometimes knock them off. Without the tee, the outlet draws from the top of the tank, right where the grease floats, and the trap sends grease to the sewer while looking perfectly fine from above. A new tee takes about thirty minutes.

    • Accurate Diagnosis, We pump and inspect before quoting, so we know exactly what failed.
    • Specialized Tools, Jetters and sewer cameras let us find the blockage and clear it instead of guessing.
    • Prevent Backups, A working trap keeps grease out of your drain lines and sewage off the kitchen floor.

    Citywide Coverage

    Emergencies do not pick a convenient address. Our trucks start the day spread out, not parked at one shop. That way the nearest plumber with a jetter takes your call, even if your kitchen is at the far end of an industrial park.

    Experienced Team

    Zurn, Watts, Schier, Thermaco and Endura traps all come through our shop. Our plumbers know where each brand tends to fail. Schier units are tough polyethylene but the lid gaskets wear, older steel Zurn and Watts traps rust out at the bottom seams, and Big Dipper automatic units need their skimmer wheel and heater checked. We order by model number, not by guess.

    Safety-First Approach

    Traffic-rated covers belong anywhere a car or truck can roll over an interceptor. A pedestrian lid will eventually break under a loaded garbage truck. We check the load rating on every outdoor lid we work on and replace undersized covers with H-20 rated castings or composite lids, because a broken lid over a deep tank is a real fall hazard. If it is unsafe, we flag it that day.

    Residential Grease Trap Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    Homes rarely need a grease trap, but some have one, usually a small plastic unit under the kitchen sink on a house with a septic system or a home bakery. Most leaks come from the lid or the threaded inlet fitting. We replace the gasket, reset the fittings with fresh thread sealant, and clear any grease plug in the outlet.     Grease Trap Odor Control & Repair in Aberdeen

    A sewer smell near the trap means gas is getting out somewhere it should not. Most of the time it is a torn lid gasket or a dry floor drain nearby. We check venting on the fixture side too, because a sink with no vent can siphon its own trap seal and let sewer gas back into the kitchen. Cleaning alone will not fix a bad gasket.

    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in Aberdeen

    Hardened grease in the inlet tee is the usual culprit when a trap backs up. We pump the trap down, then jet the inlet line with a 4,000 PSI unit until water runs free. Food solids packed against the baffle get scraped out by hand. Then we look for the reason it clogged, such as a dishwasher dumping too much hot water at once or a missing sink strainer upstream. A clog that keeps coming back nearly always has a cause you can see.

    Cost-Effective Grease Trap Solutions in Aberdeen, SD

    Pricing depends on what failed, so we quote after the trap is pumped and we can see inside it. Replacing a gasket or a tee is a modest bill. A concrete interceptor with failed walls is a different conversation, and we will lay out repair and replacement side by side with the cost of each and how many years you can expect from them. You get the numbers in writing before we start.

    Baffle Repair and Replacement in Aberdeen

    Inside a concrete gravity interceptor the baffle is usually a poured or precast wall with a crossover opening. A crumbling wall lets grease slip into the second compartment and out the outlet. We can rebuild the wall with form work and a sulfate-resistant mix or set a polyethylene baffle panel, depending on the tank. Either way, it needs a full pump-out first.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Aberdeen

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. Jetting actually scours the pipe wall clean. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. On old cast iron we lower the pressure and watch the camera so we do not open up a thin spot.

    Lid, Gasket and Riser Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    Outdoor risers take a beating from garbage trucks and the occasional forklift. A cracked riser lets surface water and grit into the interceptor. We replace damaged risers and set traffic-rated covers, H-20 rated where vehicles cross, and seal the riser joints with butyl rope so the tank stays tight. Pedestrian lids in a drive lane are a liability.

    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments. Over the years, hydrogen sulfide gas softens the concrete above the water line. We pump the tank, inspect the walls and the crossover, and repair spalled concrete with a cementitious patch or an epoxy liner rated for that service. Entry follows confined space rules.
