    What We Fix on Toilets in Aberdeen, SD

    Toilet Brands We Service in Aberdeen

    Kohler, American Standard, TOTO, Gerber and Mansfield make up most of the toilets we open, and each one uses its own flapper size or flush valve design. Put the wrong flapper on one and it will leak within weeks. We read the model number cast inside the tank wall and stock the common ones on every truck.

    Leaky Toilet Repair in Aberdeen

    Food coloring in the tank is the cheapest test we run. If color shows up in the bowl within fifteen minutes without a flush, water is leaking past the flapper or the flush valve seat, and the toilet is quietly refilling itself all day and night. Most of the time a new flapper does it, unless the seat under it is pitted or cracked.

    Toilet Flush Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    Two or three tries to get a full flush points to a flapper closing too early, a chain hooked in the wrong hole, scale in the rim jets, or a cracked overflow tube letting water bypass the bowl. You can usually see which one with the lid off. We watch a full cycle with the lid off and replace the flush valve if the overflow tube is cracked, since it cannot be patched.

    Older Toilet Brands We Service in Aberdeen, SD

    Older toilets from Eljer, Briggs, Crane and Universal Rundle still turn up in homes built before the 1990s, and their flush valves often take parts nobody stocks anymore. Some can take an adapter flapper, and we carry a few kinds. For the rest, we will let you know whether parts can still be found or if replacement is the honest answer.

    Commercial Toilet Repair in Aberdeen

    Office and retail restrooms take more flushes in a day than a house sees in a month, so the parts wear faster and fail harder. Most commercial toilets use flushometer valves instead of tanks, and those need diaphragm kits and vacuum breakers from time to time. We carry Sloan and Zurn kits. Work can be scheduled before opening or after close.

    After-Hours Toilet Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    Shutting off the angle stop buys you time, but an old multi-turn stop valve can crumble or leak at the stem when it has not been moved in fifteen years. Do not force it. If it will not close, the main shutoff for the house is the backup, and we can replace the stop with a quarter-turn valve once the water is off.

    Sensor and Flushometer Repair in Aberdeen

    Sensor flush valves fail in their own ways, from dead batteries and dirty sensor eyes to solenoids that stick open and keep a bowl running. Before replacing parts, we test the sensor and the solenoid. Batteries are the cheap fix. If the valve flushes on its own, the sensor range or a dirty lens is usually to blame.

    Emergency Toilet Repair in Aberdeen, SD
