    Water Line Repairs We Handle in Aberdeen, SD

    Galvanized Water Line Replacement in Aberdeen, SD

    Galvanized service lines were common in houses built before the 1970s, and a lot of them are still in the ground. Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a softening system is a common upgrade for homeowners. Rust and scale close the pipe from the inside. Rusty water at the first tap in the morning is a classic sign, along with pressure that drops when two fixtures run at once. Replacing the full run with copper or PEX clears all of it up. Usually a one-day job.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Aberdeen

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, either along the old route or through fresh soil next to it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    After-Hours Water Line Breaks in Aberdeen, SD

    Water lines tend to break on a Saturday night. We answer around the clock. HWR Plumbing sends a plumber who can shut off the curb stop, pump the hole and make a permanent repair on the spot, so you are not stuck with a temporary clamp until Monday. If the break is on the utility side of the meter, we tell you right away and help you get the provider out. It is more common than most people expect.

    Main Water Line Replacement in Aberdeen, SD

    Replacement beats repair once a line has leaked two or three times, or when it is galvanized or polybutylene. Patches on those materials rarely hold. We run new Type K copper or PEX from the meter to the house, typically finishing in one day whether we trench or pull the pipe. We pull the permit, set up the inspection and pressure test the new line before it is covered.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Aberdeen

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Pinholes show up where stones press on the pipe, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    PEX Water Line Repair in Aberdeen, SD

    PEX itself rarely fails underground, but the joints and the first few feet near the house take a beating. Rats and gophers sometimes chew it. A shovel or a fence post auger can nick it, and pipe left in the sun before it was buried can get brittle. We cut out the damaged section and install new pipe with crimp, clamp or expansion fittings, matched to the PEX brand already in the ground.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Aberdeen

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the house shutoff and the meter-side valve for a partly closed gate, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.

    Repair vs. Replace Your Water Line in Aberdeen, SD

    One leak on a newer copper or PEX line is a spot repair. Repeated leaks on galvanized pipe call for a new line. Age matters too, and a line past about 40 years is usually near the end no matter the material. Spot repairs often run a few hundred dollars while a full replacement costs more and resets the clock for decades. We show you the pipe we exposed.
