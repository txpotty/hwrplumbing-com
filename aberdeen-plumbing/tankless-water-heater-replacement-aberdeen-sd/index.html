    Whole-House Electric Tankless Replacement in Aberdeen

    Exterior Mount Tankless Units in Aberdeen, SD

    Exterior-mount tankless units hang on an outside wall and vent straight out the front, so there is no vent pipe to run. Sun and weather wear on the covers and wiring. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a much bigger flood. On every outdoor swap, we check freeze protection and the pipe cover. Often the simplest job we do.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Aberdeen, SD

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Their combi boilers handle both space heat and domestic water. We set up both.

    Gas Line Testing on Tankless Installs in Aberdeen, SD

    Every gas connection on a tankless gets a soap test and a manometer reading at full fire before we leave the house. Static pressure means little if it drops when the burner opens up. Both readings get taken.
