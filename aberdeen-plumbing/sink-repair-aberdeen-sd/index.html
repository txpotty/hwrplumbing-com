    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Outdoor kitchen sinks and garage utility tubs sit in the weather.     Burst Supply Line Repair in Aberdeen

    A burst line under a sink can push out several gallons a minute. Close the angle stop, or the main if the stop fails, because every minute adds water to the subfloor. Next, put towels on the floor. We replace the line and the stop and moisture-check the cabinet.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Aberdeen, SD

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.
