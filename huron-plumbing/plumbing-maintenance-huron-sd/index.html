    Backflow Preventer Testing in Huron

    Backflow devices protect the drinking water. Many water providers require yearly testing on irrigation and commercial backflow preventers, and our certified testers check each check valve and relief valve and file the results with your utility. Failed devices get rebuilt. We also check that devices are protected from heat and freezing where needed. Testing on time avoids fines.

    Tankless Water Heater Descaling in Huron

    Tankless units need descaling. Scale builds up inside the heat exchanger, especially with hard water, and the unit starts throwing error codes, running lukewarm or cutting out mid-shower long before it actually wears out. A small pump circulates descaler. We also clean the inlet screen and check venting and gas supply.

    Gas Line and Pipe Material Checks in Huron, SD

    Gas leaks are the one item we never skip. Visible gas piping, connectors and shutoffs get checked, and old flex connectors get flagged. We note pipe types. Polybutylene or old galvanized supply lines go on the report with repipe options.

    Water Softener Service in Huron

    A softener only helps if it regenerates. We check the salt level, break up any salt bridge in the brine tank, clean the brine well and injector, and run a manual regeneration to confirm the valve cycles correctly. We test the water afterward. A failed softener sends hard water through the house without warning.

    Gas Connector and Shutoff Checks in Huron

    Gas feeds water heaters, furnaces, ranges and dryers. We inspect visible gas piping, test connections with leak detector solution, make sure each appliance shutoff valve turns and replace any old or kinked flexible connectors. Repairs follow local code. If you ever smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside.

    Repipe Assessments in Huron, SD

    Polybutylene and old galvanized pipe leak more. During maintenance we identify your supply pipe material, look for pinhole leaks, rust stains and pressure drops, and tell you honestly whether repiping is something to plan for now or years from now. PEX and copper are the options. Work can be phased.

    Water Heater Flush and Inspection in Huron

    Popping or rumbling means sediment. Minerals settle on the tank bottom, trap heat and cause the popping you hear when it fires. Flushing clears it. Tankless units get a descaling flush through the service valves with a pump and descaler. Regular service helps keep the warranty valid.
