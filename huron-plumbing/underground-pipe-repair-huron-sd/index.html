    Pipe Bursting Services in Huron, SD

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. The new line takes its place. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, while a continuous HDPE pipe is pulled in behind at the same diameter or bigger. Collapsed sections are fine. Lining cannot do that.

    Trenchless Pipe Repair in Huron

    Trenchless repair replaces or relines buried pipe without opening its full length. One or two holes, not a trench. We can pull new PEX or HDPE through the old pipe path or line a sewer lateral with resin, which leaves driveways, patios and mature landscaping in place. Not every pipe qualifies. The route and condition decide it.
