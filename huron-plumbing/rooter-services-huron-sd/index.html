    Rental machines are easy to get, but they are dangerous in untrained hands. Cables can kink, whip and wrap around a wrist in a second. Rentals jam in pipes. Getting a rental cable stuck in clay pipe can mean digging it out, which costs far more than a rooter visit.

    Rooting is safe with a trained tech and the correct cable and blade. Badly cracked or collapsed pipe can be harmed by any cleaning method. Feel matters. We stop if the pipe shows damage, and we bring in the camera to look before going any further down the line.

    Chemicals sometimes open a small hole in a hair clog, but they will not touch roots or a grease plug. Repeated use can weaken old PVC joints and pit cast iron. They also burn skin. A cable takes the clog out instead of melting a narrow path through it, which keeps the drain open longer.

    A limited guarantee for a set number of days comes with most rooter work. If the line clogs again within that time, we return at no extra charge.

    Rooting uses a spinning cable with cutting blades to break up clogs and roots. Jetting is different. A jetter scours the walls with water at high pressure. Tough lines often get both, rooting to open the path and jetting to clean out what remains.
