    Hydro-Jetting Services in Huron

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Cables punch through clogs, but jetting removes the grease and scale coating the pipe, which is what keeps causing them. Restaurants need it most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    French Drain Installation in Huron

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. The pipe needs at least a 1 percent slope to a daylight outlet, a dry well or a sump, and the outlet end needs a grate so animals do not nest in it.

    Downspout and Roof Drain Solutions in Huron

    Gutters concentrate a lot of roof water into a few points, often right next to the foundation. Shifting clay and deep frost can stress buried sewer and water lines, so a camera inspection is useful when drains back up repeatedly. Underground downspout lines carry roof water to a pop-up emitter or dry well, with a cleanout for flushing. An inch of rain on a 1,000 square foot roof sheds roughly 600 gallons.     Jetting for Scale and Grease in Huron

    Jetting uses water at high pressure to strip a pipe back to its original diameter. Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a softening system is a common upgrade for homeowners. Scale and grease on the pipe wall catch debris, and stripping them off keeps the line clear far longer. Plan on about two hours.

We run a percolation check before digging, size the well for the roof or yard area feeding it, and keep it at least ten feet from the foundation and well away from any septic field.
