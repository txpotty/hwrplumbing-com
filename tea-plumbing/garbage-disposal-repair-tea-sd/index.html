    Slow Draining Disposal Repair in Tea, SD

    Jam Clearing and Motor Repair in Tea

    Bones, fruit pits and bottle caps lodge between the grind ring and the impeller lugs, and the motor stalls. A motor that stalled for a long time may have overheated, so we test it under load before calling the job done.
