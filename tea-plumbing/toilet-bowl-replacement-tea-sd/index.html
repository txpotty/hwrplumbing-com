    Cost-Effective Bowl Swaps in Tea, SD

    Swapping only the bowl saves money when the tank is newer, the fill valve and flush valve are in good shape, and the bowl is still made in your color. White bowls are the easiest to find. Discontinued colors are where bowl-only swaps fall apart, and then a full toilet costs about the same.

    Cracked or Damaged Toilet Bowl Replacement in Tea

    Porcelain that cracks below the waterline leaks every time the bowl refills, and the water usually runs down inside the base where you will not see it for weeks. Hairline cracks still count. We pull the bowl, check the flange and the subfloor, then set a matching bowl on a fresh seal.

    Brands We Match in Tea

    American Standard, Kohler, TOTO and Gerber sell bowls separately for many two-piece models, but the tank bolts, gasket style and bolt spacing have to match exactly. A near match will leak. We confirm the pairing from the model number before ordering, which avoids a second trip.

    Changing Toilet Bowl Styles in Tea

    Elongated bowls are about two inches longer than round ones and more comfortable for most adults, but they eat into the clear space code requires in front of the toilet. Small bathrooms may need a compact elongated model. The space gets measured first, so the door still closes.

    Pressure-Assist Toilet Bowl Replacement in Tea

    Pressure-assist toilets use a sealed vessel inside the tank, and the bowl is matched to that vessel's flush rate, so a bowl-only swap means finding the exact paired model. Some bowls are not sold on their own. If the pair is unavailable, a full replacement is the clean answer, and we will price both.

    Tank-to-Bowl Leak Repair and Replacement in Tea

    Drips from the bolts between the tank and bowl usually mean a flattened gasket or rusted bolts, but sometimes the porcelain around a bolt hole has cracked. The bolt holes get a close look before we quote. A gasket and bolt kit fixes the first case, and a cracked bolt hole means a new bowl with stainless or brass hardware.

    Old Toilet Upgrades in Tea, SD

    Bowls from before 1994 were built for 3.5 gallon flushes or more, and pairing one with a new low-flow tank does not work because the trapway and rim are sized for the larger volume. The tank and bowl are engineered as a pair. For an old toilet, an efficiency upgrade usually means a whole new toilet, and we will price it that way.

    Flange Repair During Bowl Replacement in Tea, SD

    The flange is the ring that anchors the bowl to the drain, and the only time anyone sees it is when the old bowl comes off. That is the moment to check it. A cracked or sunken flange gets repaired before the new bowl goes down, since a new bowl on a bad flange will rock and leak within months.
