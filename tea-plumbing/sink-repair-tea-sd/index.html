    Repair or Replace? Sink Costs in Tea, SD

    Repair is cheaper nine times out of ten.     Cracked or Loose Basin Repair in Tea

    Undermount clips let go more often than people think. Failed epoxy lets the bowl sag a little, the caulk splits, and counter water runs right into the cabinet. New clips go in and the bowl gets pulled tight. Hairline cracks in porcelain near the drain mean replacement.

    Faucet Base Leak Repair in Tea

    Water pooling around the faucet base is a different leak than a drip from the spout. That points to worn O-rings on the spout or a bad base gasket, and sometimes to a cracked faucet body that cannot be rebuilt. Each faucet style rebuilds its own way. We identify yours before ordering parts.

    Kitchen Sink Repair in Tea

    Kitchen sinks take the most abuse in the house. Strainers lose their putty seal, sprayer hoses split inside the cabinet, and disposal flanges vibrate loose over the years. We fix the part that is wet. A cracked composite or porcelain bowl is a replacement, and we will say so.

    P-Trap Repair and Replacement in Tea, SD

    The trap holds a plug of water that keeps sewer gas out of the room. Most codes want 2 to 4 inches of trap seal, and a leaking or cut-down trap can lose it and let sewer odor into the house. Bad traps get a new matched kit. Chrome traps that have rusted thin get swapped for PVC or brass.

    Hair Clog Clearing in Tea

    Hair is the bathroom sink's main problem. We pull the stopper, strip the hair mat off it, then run a small hand auger through the trap and into the wall arm instead of pouring in drain cleaner. We flush the overflow channel while we are there. That slot smells when it fills with slime.

    Recurring Sink Clog Repair in Tea, SD

    Monthly clogs have a cause past the trap. We look past the wall with a longer cable or a camera for a sag or for buildup where the sink line meets the tub. Now and then it is a plugged vent. A sink that gurgles as it drains is the giveaway.

    Overflow and Drain Flange Leak Repair in Tea, SD

    Some leaks only show up when the bowl is full. That points to the overflow gasket or the putty under the drain flange, since neither one sees water during a quick hand wash but both get soaked when you fill the sink. The drain comes out and gets resealed. Marble and some granite get silicone, because putty can stain them.
