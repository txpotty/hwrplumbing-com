    French Drain Installation in Tea

    A French drain is a gravel-filled trench with perforated pipe that collects groundwater and carries it away. We wrap the gravel in filter fabric so silt does not clog it. It needs steady fall to an outlet, and the outlet gets a grate to keep critters out.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Tea, SD

    Several drains backing up at once points to the main line, not a single fixture. Clearing runs from the outdoor cleanout with a root head, followed by a camera run to the street. No cleanout? We can install one so the next call costs less.

    Hydro-Jetting Services in Tea

    A hydro jetter sends water at several thousand psi through a nozzle that scours the full pipe wall. Unlike a cable, the jet cleans the whole pipe wall, not just a hole through the clog. Grease lines benefit most. We camera the line first to make sure the pipe is sound enough to jet.

    Channel and Trench Drains in Tea, SD

    Driveways and patios that slope toward the house can send runoff straight into a garage or a door threshold. A trench drain cut into the concrete stops it. We saw-cut the slab, set a polymer or concrete channel with a grate rated for the traffic it will see, and pipe it in solid PVC to a safe discharge point away from the foundation.
