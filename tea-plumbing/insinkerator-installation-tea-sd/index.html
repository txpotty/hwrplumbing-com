    Continuous feed is the type most kitchens have. Flip the switch and it runs until you flip it off. A batch feed unit needs its stopper locked in to run, keeping hands out of the chamber, and it needs no wall switch. We wire each one to match.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Insinkerator Installation in Tea

    Humming without grinding, leaks from the bottom of the unit, frequent reset trips and rusty water are all signs of failure. Age matters too. Once a unit passes ten years, replacement usually beats repairing a worn motor.

    Badgers are single-stage grinders with lighter motors. For normal scraps they work well. An Evolution grinds in stages with more torque and insulation, so stringy foods that stall a Badger go through it.

    A hum with no grinding is the classic one. So is water dripping from the housing. Constant jams or a reset that trips every few days usually mean a unit near the end of its life.
