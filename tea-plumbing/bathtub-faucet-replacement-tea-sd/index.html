    Budget Tub Faucet Options in Tea, SD

    Not every tub faucet needs a full valve. A new cartridge and a matching trim kit from the same brand can make a twenty-year-old single-handle faucet work and look nearly new for a fraction of what a valve replacement costs. We offer that option first. Three-handle faucets are the exception.

    Freestanding and Deck-Mount Tub Filler Installation in Tea

    Freestanding tubs need a floor-mount filler. Supplies have to rise through the floor, from a slab cut or a crawlspace, with a bracket anchored below. Deck fillers mount on the tub deck. Tub-only fillers need a temperature limit.

    Cartridge and Stem Replacement in Tea, SD

    Most dripping tub faucets need internals, not a new valve. We swap cartridges or stems and seats, greasing the O-rings so handles turn easily. Old seats need a seat wrench. About an hour, usually.

    Thermostatic Tub Valve Installation in Tea, SD

    Thermostatic valves hold the temperature you set. They sense real water temperature, not just pressure, which helps kids and older adults avoid hot spikes. Volume and temperature often get separate handles. They cost more than pressure-balance.

    Upgrading Tub Faucets in Tea, SD

    Plenty of people replace a working tub faucet for looks. Old cross handles can become a modern lever trim, and on a universal valve the wall never opens. We can match your sink finish. Delta and Moen make this simplest.

    Brand Tub Faucet Replacement in Tea

    Brand matters more on tub valves than anywhere else. Moen, Delta, Kohler, American Standard and Pfister each use their own cartridges and trim threads, and a new trim kit from the same maker as the valve usually bolts right on without touching the plumbing. Cross-brand trim seldom fits. We find the logo or the casting mark first.

    Tub Faucet Repair vs Replacement Costs in Tea, SD

    A cartridge costs much less than a valve. Replacement inside the wall makes sense when parts are discontinued, the valve body is cracked or pitted, or you want anti-scald protection on an old three-handle faucet that has none. You see both prices. Most people choose repair first.
