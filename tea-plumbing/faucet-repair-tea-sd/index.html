    Ball Faucet Repair in Tea, SD

    Ball faucets have a single lever over a domed cap, and the drip usually comes from the two small rubber seats and springs that press against the ball. Most of them just need new seats and springs. Loose handles happen too. Tightening the adjusting ring under the cap usually stops a handle leak.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Tea

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Washers harden and seats get pitted. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Commercial Faucet Repair in Tea, SD

    Restaurant and office faucets cycle hundreds of times a day, so cartridges, handle springs, metering valves and sensor solenoids wear out far faster than they do in a house. We carry commercial cartridges. We set metering faucets to run long enough for a full rinse without wasting water, and we can work after hours.

    Hard Water Faucet Repair in Tea

    Scale is the biggest reason faucet seals wear early, because the mineral crust scratches rubber and keeps ceramic discs from closing flat. Hard water speeds it up. The valve body gets cleaned while the old part is out, so the new cartridge or washer seals against a smooth surface.

    Specialty Faucet Repair in Tea, SD

    Pot fillers, laundry tub faucets and wall-mount utility faucets each have their own valve styles, and many use compression stems or quarter-turn cartridges that hardware stores do not stock. We bring the common ones. For filtered water faucets, we replace the cartridge or the check valve as needed.

    Cracked Faucet Body Repair in Tea

    Cracked faucet bodies, usually from freezing or an old brass casting that finally gave out, cannot be repaired and need a replacement faucet. Turn off the stops under the sink. We can usually install a new faucet the same day, and we will check the supply lines and stops while the water is off.

    Outdoor Hose Bib Repair in Tea

    Outdoor spigots take a beating from sun and weather. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a much bigger flood. Packing and seat washers get replaced on standard hose bibs, and we install frost-free sillcocks with the valve seat inside the warm wall when an old one has split. Every new spigot gets a vacuum breaker for backflow protection.
