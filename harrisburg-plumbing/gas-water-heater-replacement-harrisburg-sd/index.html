    Gas Water Heater Efficiency Ratings in Harrisburg

    Efficiency ratings on tank heaters are listed as UEF, uniform energy factor, and a higher number means less gas per gallon heated. Basic tanks land near 0.60 UEF. High-efficiency condensing tanks reach 0.80 or more and can qualify for rebates.

    40 and 50 Gallon Tank Replacement in Harrisburg

    Tankless Gas Conversion in Harrisburg

    Going tankless usually means a bigger gas line, since a whole-house tankless unit can draw 199,000 BTU against the 40,000 a tank burner uses. The 1/2-inch line feeding a tank is rarely enough.
