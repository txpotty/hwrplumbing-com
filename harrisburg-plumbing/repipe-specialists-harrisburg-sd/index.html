    Slab Leak Repiping in Harrisburg

    A slab leak shows up as a warm spot on the floor, a running meter or a wet patch of carpet with no visible cause. Basements are common in South Dakota homes, which puts water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where sump systems often play a role. Instead of breaking concrete to patch one pipe, we reroute new supply lines overhead through the attic and down interior walls, then abandon the old lines in the slab. No jackhammer needed.     PEX Versus Copper Repipe Pricing in Harrisburg, SD

    PEX repipes usually cost less than copper because the material is cheaper and installation goes faster. Copper costs more. Copper commonly costs 20 to 40 percent more than PEX, and some owners prefer it anyway for its long history. Our quotes for either include permit, fittings, patching and cleanup.

    Slab Leak Reroutes in Harrisburg

    When one line under the slab leaks, a full repipe is not always the first step. Rerouting one branch overhead and leaving the bad pipe in the slab is often cheaper than both a repipe and a jackhammer repair. This works well for a first leak. If the house has had several leaks, we will walk through why a full repipe usually costs less over time.

    Repiping for Hard Water in Harrisburg, SD

    Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a water softener is a common upgrade for homeowners. Hard water leaves calcium and magnesium scale inside pipe, and over years it narrows the bore and speeds up corrosion in metal lines. Pressure suffers. In hard water homes, PEX-A is often the better pick since it resists scale, and a softener loop or filter can be added during the job. Planning it now saves a second visit later.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Harrisburg, SD

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside, and over decades the inside diameter closes down to a fraction of what it was. Pressure drops slowly. Rust-colored water in the morning, poor pressure upstairs and dripping threaded joints all point to failing galvanized. We replace it with PEX or copper. Owners usually notice stronger flow the first day.
