    How We Install Faucets in Harrisburg, SD

    Bathroom Faucet Swaps in Harrisburg, SD

    Swapping a bathroom faucet usually means swapping the pop-up drain too, because the lift rod connects through the faucet body. We set the drain flange in putty, switching to silicone on cultured marble or natural stone so nothing bleeds into the top. Handles get lined up last. We set them so both close at the same angle.

    Laundry Sink Faucet Installation in Harrisburg

    Laundry sinks get a faucet with a threaded spout. Deck or wall models go in with a built-in vacuum breaker, because a hose sitting in dirty wash water can siphon back into the supply. We clip the supply lines so nothing rubs.

    Commercial Faucet Installation in Harrisburg

    Commercial restrooms need faucets that hold up to hundreds of uses a day. We install sensor faucets and wrist-blade handles for hand sinks, plus pre-rinse units in commercial kitchens, all to current accessibility rules. We can come after you close. Your contractor or property manager gets the fixture specs for inspection.

    Utility Sink Faucet Installation in Harrisburg, SD

    Utility tubs see hot water and harsh cleaners. A utility faucet needs a brass body and a firm mount, because people yank hoses off it, and a loose faucet cracks its own supply connection. The spout gets a vacuum breaker. We check these joints twice, since a garage tub can drip for months before anyone notices.

    Pot Filler Installation in Harrisburg

    A pot filler needs its own cold line. The new cold line comes up inside the wall behind the cooktop, with an accessible shutoff valve so you can service the filler later. The arm folds flat when not in use. The line is tested under pressure before anyone tiles over it.

    Commercial-Style Kitchen Faucets in Harrisburg

    Commercial-style spring spout faucets are tall. Many spring spouts stand over 20 inches, so we measure up to the window sill or cabinet before you buy. Any extra sink hole can hold a soap pump or side sprayer. We set the hose weight where the spout docks without slapping the cabinet.

    High-End Faucet Installation in Harrisburg, SD

    High-end faucets ship with more parts in the box. Premium lines from Brizo and Grohe often use a separate valve body plus finish-matched trim that has to be assembled in a set order under the deck. We use padded wrenches on the finish. Brushed gold and matte black scratch easily, so nothing bare and metal touches the trim.

    A faucet dripping once a second wastes a few thousand gallons a year. When the body itself is corroded or the brand stopped making parts, replacing it beats paying for a third cartridge swap.
