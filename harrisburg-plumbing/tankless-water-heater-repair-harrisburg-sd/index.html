    Tankless Descaling and Flushing in Harrisburg

    Rinnai shows an LC code and Navien posts a scale warning when the exchanger runs hot from mineral buildup. We connect a circulation pump to the service valves, run descaler through the exchanger for about an hour, then flush and clean the inlet screen. Yearly is right for most hard-water homes.

    Noritz Tankless Water Heater Repair in Harrisburg, SD

    Noritz heaters hold up well, but their flow sensors and vent connections still wear out. A code 90 points to combustion trouble, often a dirty fan or a restricted intake.     Flow Sensor and Thermistor Repair in Harrisburg

    Tankless heaters start when the flow sensor sees about half a gallon a minute, then trim the burner using thermistor readings.
