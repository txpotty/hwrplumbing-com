    Drainage System Integration in Harrisburg, SD

    Water has to go somewhere once a wall is sealed. Footing drains, interior drain tile and French drains in the yard all do the same job at different depths. We match the drain to where the water comes from, which might be a high water table, a yard that slopes toward the house, or a gutter dumping at the corner. We extend downspouts at least four feet from the wall.

    Commercial Walls Waterproofing in Harrisburg

    Commercial basements, elevator pits and mechanical rooms in Harrisburg take on water the same way houses do, just with more at stake. One wet electrical room can close a business for days. We schedule around operating hours, work with property managers on access, and follow the waterproofing specs written into the building plans for below-grade walls. Downtime stays short.

    Crawl Space Vapor Barriers in Harrisburg

    Bare dirt in a crawl space releases gallons of water vapor a day into the air under your floor. A 10 mil or heavier polyethylene barrier, lapped 12 inches at the seams and taped, cuts that off and keeps the joists and subfloor dry. We run it up the foundation walls and hold it in place with termination strip. It is a cheap fix that makes a big difference.

    Commercial Wall Waterproofing in Harrisburg

    Restaurant kitchens, gym locker rooms and salon wash stations put water on walls all day. We waterproof commercial showers, mop sink walls and restroom wet walls with membranes rated for constant water exposure, and we repair the supply and drain piping behind them if demolition turns up a leak. We can work after hours.

    Cost-Effective Waterproofing Solutions in Harrisburg, SD

    Not every wet wall needs a $15,000 excavation. Sometimes the fix is a downspout extension and regrading the soil so it slopes away from the house, a drop of about six inches in the first ten feet, and that costs a few hundred dollars. We start with the cheapest fix that will actually work. You get the options in writing, side by side.

    Crawl Space Encapsulation in Harrisburg

    Encapsulation seals the whole crawl space, walls and floor, with a reinforced poly liner. We run 12 to 20 mil liner up the walls, tape every seam, seal around piers and pipe penetrations, and often add a dehumidifier to keep relative humidity below 60 percent. Long, cold winters and hot summers put South Dakota plumbing through wide temperature swings that expand and contract pipes and fittings every year. Wood rot slows to a stop. Drier air under the floor also cuts down on the musty smell in the house.

    Plumbing Leak Behind Walls in Harrisburg

    A drip from a shower valve cartridge, a sweated copper stub or a loose drain slip nut can soak a wall cavity for months before paint bubbles. We find it with a moisture meter, a thermal camera that shows the cool wet spot, and a pressure test on the supply lines. Then we cut a clean access hole. We replace the leaking fitting or pipe section. We pull wet insulation and run fans until the cavity reads dry.

    Shower Wall Waterproofing in Harrisburg

    Tile and grout are not waterproof. Water passes through grout every time you shower, so the layer behind the tile, a membrane over cement board or a foam board system, is what actually keeps the studs dry. We tear out failed tile, install new backer, and apply Schluter Kerdi, RedGard or Hydro Ban at the thickness the manufacturer calls for. Corners, niches and valve holes get extra fabric or preformed pieces.

    Damp proofing is usually a thin spray-on asphalt coat. It slows soil moisture but will not stop water that is pushing against the wall under pressure. Waterproofing uses thicker membranes, drainage boards and footing drains rated to stop liquid water, and that is what a basement sitting in wet soil or a high water table calls for.

    Yes. Our installs carry a written warranty on labor and materials, and the length depends on the system we put in, while many membranes and injection resins also come with a manufacturer warranty. The details are spelled out in your service agreement.

    Most systems we install should go 20 to 30 years or longer. Life span comes down to the method we use, the quality of the install and the soil conditions around your house. The sump pump will need replacing well before that. Most pumps last seven to ten years.

    Bubbling paint is a big one. Soft drywall near the floor, stained baseboards, a musty smell and white crust on block or stucco all point to moisture, and in a shower, loose tiles or cracked grout usually mean water is already behind the wall. A moisture meter confirms it in minutes.

    Schluter Kerdi, Custom RedGard and Laticrete Hydro Ban cover most of our showers.     It depends on the wall and the site.
