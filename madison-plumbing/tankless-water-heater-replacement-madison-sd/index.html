    Picking a Tankless for Your Madison, SD Home

    Condensing Tankless Replacement in Madison

    Condensing tankless units like the Navien NPE or Rinnai Sensei pass 90 percent efficiency by pulling extra heat out of the exhaust. They vent through PVC. Acidic condensate goes through a neutralizer and then to an approved drain. Long, cold winters and hot summers put South Dakota plumbing through wide temperature swings that expand and contract pipes and fittings every year. The savings carry through every season because hot water use does.

    Tank to Tankless Conversion in Madison, SD

    Converting from a tank to tankless takes a full day in most homes. The work usually includes a larger gas line, new venting through a wall or roof, and a new spot on the wall for the unit. We cap or remove the old flue.

    Commercial Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Madison, SD

    Commercial tankless setups in restaurants and offices often use two or more units on a manifold, with a controller that splits the load. One bad unit can come out while the others keep running. Health code sets the hot water temperature at the hand sinks and dish areas.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Madison, SD

    Navien NPE units are condensing, and the A models have a built-in recirculation pump and buffer tank that cut down on cold-water sandwiches. Navien also makes combi units that heat the house and the water. Both are in our wheelhouse.

    Gas Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    A gas tankless unit at full fire can pull 199,000 BTU, roughly five times what a 40 gallon tank burner uses. The gas line usually has to be 3/4-inch or larger. Pipe length and meter capacity get checked against the sizing tables, and we confirm pressure at full fire. An undersized line causes ignition faults.

    Point-of-Use Electric Tankless in Madison

    Point-of-use electric tankless heaters under a sink are a different animal from whole-house units. A small under-sink unit needs one 20 or 30 amp circuit and suits a distant bath or break room. Most take under two hours.

    Tank to Tankless Conversions in Madison, SD

    Pulling a 50 gallon tank out of a garage frees up floor space and ends standby heat loss. Gas is the catch, since tankless units usually need 3/4-inch pipe or larger and a new vent. We check the meter size and pipe run before quoting, and we cap or reroute the old flue and seal the roof penetration if it is no longer needed. Plan on a full day.

    Emergency Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Madison

    No hot water from a tankless usually shows up as an error code on the controller, and the code tells us half of what we need to know. A photo of the error code helps a lot. If the heat exchanger is leaking, replacement is the fix, and we can often have a new unit running the same or next day.
