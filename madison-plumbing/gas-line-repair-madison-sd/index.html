    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up.     CSST Repair in Madison

    Corrugated stainless steel tubing is a flexible gas pipe with a plastic jacket, often yellow, that runs through attics and walls in newer homes. It can be damaged. Nails from a remodel, rodents chewing the jacket and nearby lightning strikes are the usual causes, and a damaged run has to be cut out and spliced with the manufacturer's own fittings. We check bonding on every CSST job. The system should be bonded to the electrical grounding system with a proper clamp and conductor, per the manufacturer's instructions and local code.
