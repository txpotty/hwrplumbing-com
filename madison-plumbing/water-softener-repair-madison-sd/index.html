    Resin Bed Problems in Madison

    Fouled resin is the usual reason a softener regenerates on schedule but still sends hard water to every faucet in the house. Iron and sediment coat the beads and block the exchange. Depending on the test, the resin gets cleaned or replaced. New resin restores full capacity.

    Water Softener Leak Detection and Repair in Madison, SD

    Cracked tanks, loose fittings and failed O-rings are behind most softener leaks. A small drip still wastes water and damages flooring. We find the source, whether it is the bypass, the valve-to-tank seal or the drain connection, and replace the seal or fitting that failed. We test it under pressure.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Madison, SD

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. A minor drip still wastes water and damages floors and cabinets. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. A cracked mineral tank usually means replacement.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Madison

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night.     Salt Bridge & Brine Tank Repair in Madison

    Salt bridges form when a hard crust builds across the brine tank and the salt below it stops dissolving into the water. The tank looks full, but the softener cannot make brine. We break the bridge, clean out the mushy salt and check the float and brine well. A cracked brine tank gets swapped for a new one.

    Salt Bridge and Brine Tank Service in Madison, SD

    The tank looks full but makes no brine. A hard crust has formed and is keeping the salt from dissolving. Bridges get broken up, mushy salt gets cleaned out and the float, brine well and safety valve get checked. In a hot or damp garage, switching to pellets cuts down on bridging.
