    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Madison, SD

    Calcium scale is what kills most tankless units early. Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a softening system is a common upgrade for homeowners. Scale coats the heat exchanger, cuts efficiency and triggers error codes, and left alone it can crack the exchanger and end the warranty claim before it starts. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.
