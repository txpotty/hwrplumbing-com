    PEX Pipe Installation in Madison, SD

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. On a whole-house repipe that means fewer joints hidden behind drywall and fewer places for a slow leak to start rotting a stud. It handles a freeze better than rigid pipe. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a far larger flood.
