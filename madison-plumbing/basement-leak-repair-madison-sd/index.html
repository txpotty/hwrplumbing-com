    Crack Injection for Basements in Madison

    Injection works on poured concrete walls, not block. Ports go along the crack, the face gets sealed, and resin goes in from the bottom up until it fills the full depth. Epoxy bonds the two sides. For a crack that is actively wet, polyurethane works better since it reacts with water.

    Wall Leak Solutions in Madison, SD

    Damp walls without a crack often point to water soaking through porous block or concrete. A simple foil test tells you whether it is seepage or condensation: tape a square of foil to the wall for a day and see which side gets wet. Condensation needs a dehumidifier. Seepage needs drainage outside or a channel inside.

    Wall and Slab Seepage Repair in Madison

    Interior Drainage Systems in Madison

    Where digging outside is not practical, an interior drain system does the same job from inside. A strip of slab comes out along the walls, perforated pipe goes in on stone, and it all drains to a sump. The wall gets a dimpled membrane. Seepage runs down behind it and into the pipe.
