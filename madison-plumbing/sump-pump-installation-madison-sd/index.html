    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Madison

    Combination units pair a primary pump and a battery backup on one pre-plumbed discharge assembly. Because everything comes assembled on a single riser, they fit into an 18-inch pit that would be too tight for two separate pumps and two check valves. That saves some labor. We set them in homes that want two layers without a bigger pit.

    High Water Alarms and Smart Monitoring in Madison

    A high water alarm is a float switch that sounds when the pit rises past normal. Wi-Fi models send a text too. Landlords like them. We add them to new installs and to pumps already in place.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Systems in Madison, SD

    Backup systems we install use sealed AGM batteries that do not need water. A smart charger keeps the battery topped off and sounds an alarm if the battery weakens, the primary fails or the water rises past the backup float. Expect a battery swap every four to five years. Finished basements get the most out of them.

    Sump Pump Installation Cost in Madison, SD

    Price depends mostly on whether a pit already exists. A swap in an existing pit is the low end. Battery backups and alarms add to the total. Your written quote shows each option and its price.

    Sump Pump Replacement in Madison, SD

    Floats stick and motors seize when a pump sits idle for months between storms. We pull the old pump and inspect the pit. Undersized pumps get upsized. We fill the pit and watch it run.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Madison, SD

    Storms knock out power at the exact moment the pit is filling fastest. The backup pump gets its own float and its own check valve, and it runs off a sealed 12-volt battery when the outlet goes dead. A good battery carries it through most outages. We mount the charger on the wall, off the floor.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Madison

    Submersible pumps live at the bottom of the pit with a sealed, oil-cooled motor. They run quieter and move more water. Lids close flat over them. Each install gets a sealed lid and a grommet around the cord to keep out radon and odor.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Madison, SD

    Pedestal pumps put the motor on a column above the pit, so the motor never sits in water. That makes service easy. They are louder than submersibles. We set the float rod and the discharge so the pump cycles cleanly.
