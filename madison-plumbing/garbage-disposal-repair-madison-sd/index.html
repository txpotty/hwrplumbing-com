    Humming Disposal Repair in Madison

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both.     Leaking Disposal Fix in Madison, SD

    When the drip starts at the top, the putty under the sink flange is usually dried out.     Jams and minor leaks usually cost far less than a new unit. If the disposal is past eight to ten years old, leaks from the body or has a failing motor, replacement is usually the better value, and we give you both prices when it is close. You make the call.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Madison, SD

    InSinkErator, Waste King and Moen each use different mounts, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit.     Leak Detection and Repair in Madison, SD

    There are four usual leak points on a disposal: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Three of the four are fixable. A leak from the housing means it is time for a new unit.

    Grinding Noise Repair in Madison

    A metal-on-metal grind may mean a loose mount, a foreign object or worn impellers. We inspect the chamber, tighten the mounting ring and remove any debris, then run the unit empty to hear whether the motor itself is making the noise. Bad bearings mean replacement.
