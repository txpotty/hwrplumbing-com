    New Construction Toilet Installation in Madison

    On new builds we set toilets after the finish floor is down, never before. The flange should sit on top of the finished floor. Set it too early and the tile setter has to cut around it, which leaves the flange low and forces a thick seal or an extender later on. Each bowl gets a fresh seal.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Madison

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. There is no tank to refill. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    Bathroom Remodel Toilet Installation in Madison, SD

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Madison, SD

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box.
