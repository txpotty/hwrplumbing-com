    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Madison

    Hard water is the norm across much of South Dakota, and many groundwater sources carry dissolved minerals like calcium, magnesium, sulfate and iron. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Water Heater Safety Inspection in Madison

    A water heater stores a lot of hot water under pressure, so safety items matter. Our checks include the relief valve and its discharge line, the expansion tank and the drain pan. We check gas units for draft. A heater that backdrafts can spill combustion gases into the house, so we test the draft at the vent hood and note any problems right away.

    Preventive Maintenance Checks in Madison, SD

    A maintenance check is a lighter version of a full inspection for owners who want to stay ahead of problems.
