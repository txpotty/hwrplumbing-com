    Frequently Asked Questions About Drain Repairs in Madison

    Trap and slip-joint repairs typically cost $150 to $350. Replacing pipe costs more. A section of drain pipe usually runs $400 to $1,200, and cast iron stack replacement can range from $2,000 to $6,000 with drywall, flooring and access driving most of the spread. You get the price in writing first.

    We install PVC and ABS for most new drain work. Cast iron where needed. Cast iron still makes sense in some multi-story walls where noise matters, and we repair existing copper, galvanized and cast iron drains with materials that match local code. All materials are code-listed.

    Age and corrosion lead the list. Settling ground plays a part. Tree roots, temperature swings, heavy loads over buried pipe, plus bad installation such as skipped primer or unsupported runs, can all break a drain early. Most failures have more than one cause.

    A dry trap is the most common cause. Unused fixtures dry out. A cracked drain fitting, a failed wax ring under the toilet or a blocked vent all leak gas too, and a smoke test usually finds the source in minutes. The fix is often quick.

    Keep grease out of the kitchen sink. Use hair screens. Flush only toilet paper, run hot water after greasy dishes, and schedule a professional cleaning every year or two if your home is older or has a history of clogs. Hard water homes may need it more often.

    Cleaning removes a blockage from a sound pipe. Repair changes the pipe itself. A crack, a sag, a broken fitting or a missing vent will keep causing clogs no matter how often the line gets cleaned, which is why recurring clogs usually call for a fix instead of another cleaning. We check on video.

    Clearing a simple clog takes an hour or two. Repairs run longer. Replacing a section of main line or opening a wall to swap a cast iron stack can take a full day or more, depending on access and whether a permit inspection is needed. The quote includes a time estimate.

    Unclogging a drain does not need one. Bigger changes might. Replacing a stack, relocating a drain or replacing a section of the building drain in Madison may require a plumbing permit, depending on local rules and how much of the system is being changed. The permit is on us if needed.
