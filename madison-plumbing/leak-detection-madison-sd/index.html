    Underground Water Line Leak Detection in Madison, SD

    The buried line from your meter to the house can leak for months without surfacing, especially in old galvanized pipe. Green grass in a dry spell is a clue.     Gas Line Leak Locating in Madison

    Gas leaks are found by test, not by nose. Our plumbers pressure test the system with a gauge, then check each joint with a detector and soap solution. We also walk the yard line, since a dead strip of grass over a buried gas pipe can signal a leak that has run for a long time. Utility coordination is included.

    Fixture and Appliance Leaks in Madison, SD

    One running toilet can waste hundreds of gallons daily. Worn faucet cartridges, softener valves and water heater connectors add more.     Leaks in Walls and Ceilings in Madison, SD

    A ceiling stain right below a bathroom usually points to a shower valve, a drain joint or a toilet wax ring. Our job is figuring out which. Thermal cameras show the cool wet area, a moisture meter confirms it and a borescope through a small hole lets us see the actual pipe before any big opening is made.

    Pool Leak Detection in Madison

    A pool losing more than a quarter inch a day beyond evaporation likely has a leak somewhere. We start with a bucket test. Then we pressure test the suction and return lines, use an underwater hydrophone to listen, and dye test around the skimmer, lights and main drain so we can point to the exact fitting or crack.
