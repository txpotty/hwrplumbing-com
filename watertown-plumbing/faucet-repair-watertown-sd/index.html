    Common Faucet Repairs in Watertown, SD

    Dripping Faucet Repair in Watertown

    One drip a second adds up to more than 3,000 gallons a year, roughly the water used in 180 showers. It shows up on the bill. The worn washer or cartridge gets tracked down and replace it, then check the cabinet below for swelling or stains from the slow leak.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Watertown

    Two-handle faucets with separate hot and cold handles are often compression faucets, which seal by pressing a rubber washer down onto a brass seat inside the valve. Over time the washer hardens and the seat wears rough. We replace the washer and dress or replace the seat with a seat wrench, and the packing gets redone if the stem leaks.

    Low Water Flow Faucet Repair in Watertown

    Sputtering flow, where the water spits and surges, often means air or debris in the line after a water main repair, or a partly clogged cartridge screen. A flush of the lines usually fixes it. Pull-down faucets also have check valves in the hose that can stick, and we clean or replace those.

    Delta and Moen Faucet Repair in Watertown, SD

    Delta and Moen are the faucets we repair most, and both back their cartridges with lifetime warranties for the original owner on many models. Ask us to check. Kohler, Pfister and American Standard use their own cartridges, and we stock the common ones for each brand.

    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Watertown, SD

    Single-handle faucets from Moen, Delta, Kohler and Pfister mostly use replaceable cartridges, and a worn one shows up as a drip, a stiff handle or water that will not hold a steady temperature. The part number depends on the brand and model. We match it. A Moen 1225 cartridge swap takes about thirty minutes.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Watertown

    Ceramic disc faucets last a long time, but grit from a water main repair or scale from hard water can scratch the discs or wear out the rubber inlet seals underneath. Inlet seals cost very little. A scratched disc means a new cartridge, since the two discs are lapped to fit each other and cannot be resurfaced.

    Tub Spout and Diverter Repair in Watertown, SD

    Water still running from the tub spout while the shower is on means the diverter is worn, either the gate inside the spout or a diverter stem in the valve. There are slip-on and threaded spouts. We measure the pipe stub and bring the right style, and the fix gives you full pressure at the showerhead again.

    Faucet Repair Versus Replacement in Watertown, SD

    A new cartridge usually costs well under the price of a new faucet and can add years of life, as long as the faucet body is sound and parts are still made. Cracked or discontinued faucets are different. We give you both prices so the choice is yours.
