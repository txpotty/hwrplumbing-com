    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Watertown, SD

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Uncoated brass connectors from decades ago crack at the flare fittings and should be replaced on sight. New coated connectors go in. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Corroded Gas Pipe Replacement in Watertown

    Buried steel gas line corrodes once its coating cracks, and moisture, roots and ground movement speed it up. Shifting clay and deep frost can strain buried sewer and water lines, so a camera inspection is useful when drains back up repeatedly. Rusted buried steel gets replaced with polyethylene and tracer wire, and we use a listed transition riser where it rises to the house. Above-ground runs stay in steel. Everything is tested before the trench is backfilled, so a bad joint gets caught while it is still easy to reach.

    Gas Leak Repair at Fittings and Valves in Watertown

    Most residential gas leaks are at a threaded joint, a valve stem or a flexible connector, not in the middle of a pipe. That keeps repairs small. We back out the leaking joint, clean the threads, apply gas-rated thread sealant and reassemble it, or replace the fitting or valve if the threads are damaged. Every repair gets a soap test and a system pressure test before we leave.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Watertown, SD

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. It takes about an hour. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Summer thunderstorms can drop a lot of rain in a short time, pushing water back through floor drains when sewer lines are overloaded.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Watertown

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. We tighten, reseal or replace fittings and sections with new pipe and gas-rated thread sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Heavily rusted pipe in garages, crawlspaces or outside walls is replaced before it fails, and we pressure test the full system afterward.

    Gas Line Relocation and Upgrades in Watertown, SD

Then we size the new piping so every appliance in the house still gets full pressure at the same time.
