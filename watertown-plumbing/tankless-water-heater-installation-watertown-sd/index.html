    Hard Water Protection for Tankless Units in Watertown, SD

    Calcium scale is the most common reason tankless heaters fail. Hard water is the norm across much of South Dakota, and many groundwater sources carry dissolved minerals like calcium, magnesium, sulfate and iron. Once scale coats the heat exchanger, efficiency drops and error codes start, and over time it can crack the exchanger and void the warranty. A yearly flush prevents it. We install isolation valves for that.

    Rinnai Tankless Water Heater Installation in Watertown, SD

    Rinnai has been making tankless heaters for decades, and parts are easy to get. We install both condensing and non-condensing Rinnai models, and the right one depends on where the vent can go and how much you want to spend up front. The non-condensing ones need stainless vent pipe. Condensing units vent in PVC and drain condensate.

    Navien Tankless Water Heater Installation in Watertown

    Navien builds condensing tankless units with stainless heat exchangers, and many models include a built-in recirculation pump. That pump can push hot water toward a far bathroom on a schedule, which shortens the wait at the tap without adding a separate pump under the sink. Navien vents in PVC. There is a condensate line to route as well.

    Recirculation Systems for Tankless Heaters in Watertown

    Tankless heaters can take a few extra seconds to get hot water to a far bathroom, since the burner fires only after it senses flow. A recirculation pump on a timer or a motion sensor keeps the hot line primed, so the wait at the farthest sink drops from a minute to a few seconds. It saves water too.
