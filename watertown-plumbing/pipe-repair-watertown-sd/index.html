    PEX and Copper Pipe Repair in Watertown, SD

    PEX and copper fail in different ways. Copper pins. Copper thins from the inside and develops pinholes, while PEX usually fails at fittings or where it was kinked during installation, so the truck carries tools and fittings for both. We test before closing up.

    Galvanized Pipe Repair in Watertown

    Frozen and Burst Line Repair in Watertown

    Pipes in exterior walls, attics and crawlspaces freeze first. Ice splits the pipe. The split usually does not leak until the ice thaws, which is why many burst pipes are found hours after the cold snap. We repair the split and add insulation. We can add heat cable where needed.
