    Backflow Prevention Assembly Service in Watertown

    A backflow assembly keeps water from your building, including irrigation lines, boiler water and chemical feeds, from flowing back into the public supply. Most providers want a yearly test. We test RPZ, double check and PVB assemblies with calibrated gauges and send the results straight to the water provider. A failed test usually means new rubber and springs, not a whole new assembly. That is why the truck carries Watts, Wilkins and Febco kits.

    Slab Leak Detection for Businesses in Watertown

    A slab leak under a store or office floor often shows up as a warm spot, a running water meter with everything off, or a crack in floor tile. Basements are common in South Dakota homes, which puts water heaters, softeners and main drains below grade where sump systems often play a role. We confirm it with a meter test and pressure test, then use acoustic listening gear and thermal imaging to pinpoint the leak within a foot or two. Then we decide. Some leaks are best fixed with a small cut through the slab, while others are cheaper to abandon and reroute overhead. We price both so you can compare.
