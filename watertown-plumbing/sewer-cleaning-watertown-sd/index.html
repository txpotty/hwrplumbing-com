    Sewer Camera Inspections in Watertown, SD

    On a repeat backup, the camera goes in first. We would rather look than guess. A waterproof color camera on a push rod shows the inside of the line live on a screen, so we can see the clog, measure its distance from the cleanout and spot any crack or sag.

    Cast Iron Sewer Cleaning in Watertown, SD

    Cast iron rots from the inside. Newer construction mostly uses PEX supply lines and plastic drains, which handle freeze and thaw cycles better than rigid pipe. Scale builds on top while the bottom channels out. Because a heavy cutter can punch through weak iron, we use chain descalers and a lower-pressure jet, then check the wall thickness on camera.

    Jetting a Sewer Line in Watertown, SD

    Grease and scale are jetting work. A cable only scratches them. The rear jets drive the hose forward while the spray scours the wall, so the line ends up much closer to its original size.

    Sewer Cleaning Versus Sewer Repair in Watertown, SD

    Cleaning cannot fix a broken pipe. It only removes what is inside. If your sewer backs up every few months, the camera usually shows a belly, a crack or a collapsed section, and we compare the yearly cost of cleanings to a spot repair, a liner or a replacement, with the numbers in writing.

    Main Sewer Line Clearing in Watertown

    The main carries everything out of the house. When it stops, nothing drains. Our machines push a cutting head through the full length of the lateral until it breaks through and flow returns, which is common work in the older homes around Watertown.

    After-Hours Sewer Backups in Watertown

    Backups happen at bad times. We answer anyway. The after-hours rate is quoted on the phone before we dispatch, and the tech arrives ready to clear the main, set up a wet vac and photograph the damage for your insurance company.

    Tree Root Removal in Watertown, SD Sewers

    Roots get in through tiny gaps. Once inside, they grow fast. We cut the root ball with a saw head sized for the pipe, then flush the leftovers with a jetter when the growth is heavy, and a camera shows where they are getting in.

    Preventative Sewer Maintenance in Watertown

    Roots come back after cutting. Plan on regrowth in 12 to 18 months. Putting a root-prone main on a yearly cleaning schedule keeps the growth small enough that it never stops the line, and it costs far less than an after-hours emergency call.
