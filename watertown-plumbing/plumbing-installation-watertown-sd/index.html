    Natural Gas and Propane Lines in Watertown, SD

    Natural gas and propane run at different pressures, and the pipe sizing changes with them. Our licensed plumbers size each run from the appliance BTU ratings and the developed length, then choose black iron or CSST to suit the route. CSST must be bonded. Skip the bond and a nearby lightning strike can arc through the tubing, which is one more reason gas work is not a weekend project.

    Water Service Line Installation in Watertown, SD

    New water service lines run from the meter to the house, and the old one usually comes out in the same trench. Soils in South Dakota range from glacial deposits in the east to clay rich ground farther west that swells when wet and shrinks when dry. The trenching equipment we bring depends on what we find on site. A new main shutoff goes in where the line enters. Depth and bedding protect the pipe. Pressure and flow get tested before the trench is closed.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Watertown, SD

    PEX bends around corners, shrugs off corrosion and needs far fewer fittings than copper. In a repipe, fewer fittings means fewer hidden joints and fewer chances for a leak to soak a wall. It can also stretch when water freezes. When a pipe does freeze, thawing it slowly and checking for splits before the water warms back up can prevent a far larger flood. That stretch is not a license to skip insulation, so exterior-wall and crawl-space runs still get wrapped.

    Gas Line Installation in Watertown, SD

    A new range or an outdoor kitchen all start with the same math: total BTU load and the length of the run. We size the pipe from that, pick black iron or CSST, and trench where the line goes underground. Every joint gets a soap test. Then the whole line holds pressure on a gauge before the inspector sees it.

    PEX Pipe Installation and Repiping in Watertown

    Pinhole leaks in copper keep coming back once the pipe walls start to thin. Repiping in PEX stops the cycle. We run either a manifold with home runs to each fixture, which balances pressure, or a trunk-and-branch layout when the walls make home runs impractical. Access holes are kept small and patched, or left square and clean for your drywall contractor.

    Water Heater Replacement in Watertown

    Most tank heaters give out between 8 and 12 years, sooner with heavy sediment. We install 40 and 50 gallon tanks, plus 75 gallon units for big households, from Rheem and Bradford White, with new flex connectors, a relief line piped to code and a vent check on every job. An expansion tank check is part of every job. One visit covers most installs.

    Backflow Preventer Installation in Watertown

    Irrigation systems and fire sprinkler lines are the usual reasons a home needs a backflow preventer. The device stops lawn water and fertilizer from siphoning back into the drinking supply when a main break or hydrant flush drops street pressure. Type matters. We match the assembly to the hazard and the local rule, then test it before we leave.

    Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Watertown, SD

    A basement bathroom sits below the sewer line, so gravity cannot carry the waste out. The fix is a sealed basin with an ejector pump that lifts waste to the main. We size the pump and basin to the fixtures it serves, vent the basin to code, and put a check valve and a full-port shutoff on the discharge so the pump can be pulled for service. Brands we trust here are Liberty and Zoeller.
