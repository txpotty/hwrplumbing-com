    Propane Gas Line Service in Rapid Valley, SD

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.

    Gas Appliance Line Installation in Rapid Valley, SD

    A new range, dryer or water heater needs a gas line sized for its BTU rating and the length of the run. Undersized lines starve burners. We install the branch line, add an accessible shutoff valve and a sediment trap where the code calls for one, then connect the appliance with a new coated flexible connector. Leaking existing lines get repaired during the same trip.

    Appliance Connector and Shutoff Repair in Rapid Valley

    Old flexible connectors, especially uncoated brass ones, crack and leak with age. New coated stainless connectors go on, sized to the appliance, with a reachable shutoff valve if the old one is stuck or missing. This is a quick fix. Furnaces, water heaters, ranges and dryers each need a sound connection, and most need a drip leg before the appliance valve.

    Pool Heater, Fire Pit and Grill Lines in Rapid Valley, SD

    Backyard gas lines for pool heaters, fire pits and built-in grills face sun, weather and irrigation water that shorten their life. A pool heater can pull 250,000 BTU or more, which can rob gas from the furnace and water heater if the line was sized for a grill. Sizing is the first thing we check. Repairs and new runs are tested, buried at code depth and marked with tracer wire.

    Interior Black Iron Pipe Repair in Rapid Valley

    Threaded black iron pipe can leak at joints after years of vibration or settling. Our plumbers reseal or replace the leaking fittings and sections using new pipe and approved sealant. Rusty runs get swapped out. Pipe in a garage, crawlspace or exterior wall that shows heavy rust gets replaced before it turns into a leak, and the whole system gets a pressure test after the repair.

    Permits and Code Compliance for Gas Work in Rapid Valley, SD

    Most gas piping work beyond a simple connector swap needs a permit in Rapid Valley, SD. The inspector checks pipe sizing, material, support spacing, shutoff locations, sediment traps and the pressure test result before signing off. The permit is on us. Doing it this way keeps your insurance clean and gives you a record of the repair if you sell the house later.

    CSST Repair and Bonding in Rapid Valley, SD

    Newer homes often have corrugated stainless steel tubing running through the attic. CSST can be damaged by nails, rodents or lightning, and older yellow-jacket CSST needs proper bonding to the electrical grounding system to reduce the risk of a lightning arc. Bonding is a short job. We repair damaged sections with manufacturer-approved fittings and verify bonding before we leave. Drought years are also part of the cycle here, and dry, shrinking soil can crack foundations and disturb buried lines.
