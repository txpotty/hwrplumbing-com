    TAC Salt-Free Conditioners in Rapid Valley

    Template-assisted crystallization, or TAC, is the salt-free media with real independent test data behind it, and it is the only kind we install. The tank goes on the main line downstream of the filter, with no power or drain needed. We do not sell clamp-on magnet devices.

    Urgent Softener Problems in Rapid Valley

    Not every softener failure is urgent. Water on the floor from the valve or an overflowing brine tank does count, and those calls get moved up. Bypass it until we arrive.

    Whole-Home Softener Placement in Rapid Valley, SD

    Every tap in the house, including the shower and the water heater, gets soft water when the softener sits on the main line ahead of everything. We typically leave the hose bibs on hard water. Why soften water for the lawn?

    Fleck Valve Programming in Rapid Valley, SD

    Programming is where most Fleck replacements go wrong. A Fleck needs hardness, household size and regen time entered right, otherwise it cycles too often or runs out of capacity. It is programmed before we pack up.

    Softener Replacement on a Budget in Rapid Valley, SD

    Capacity and valve brand set most of the price. A basic 32,000-grain metered unit costs far less than a twin-tank Kinetico, and for a small household on moderately hard water it may be all you need. We show both options side by side.

    Dual Tank Softener Systems in Rapid Valley

    Twin-tank systems keep one tank in service while the other regenerates, so you never get hard water during a middle-of-the-night regeneration. Kinetico runs them without electricity, using water flow to drive the valve. The price is higher.

    Emergency Softener Replacement in Rapid Valley, SD

    A softener that sticks in regeneration can run water to the drain for hours, and a cracked bypass or tank can flood a garage floor. First step, turn the bypass handle. We can usually replace a leaking or stuck system within a day or two, and same day if we have the size on the truck.

    Softener Failure Diagnosis in Rapid Valley, SD

    A clogged injector, a dead motor or a wrong setting can make a good softener look finished. We check those first and test hardness before and after the unit, so you know whether it is softening at all. Obsolete valves are another story.
