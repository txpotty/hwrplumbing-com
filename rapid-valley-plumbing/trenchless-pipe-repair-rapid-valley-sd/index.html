    CIPP Lining for Clay and Cast Iron in Rapid Valley

    Older Rapid Valley homes often have clay or cast iron sewer pipe. Both fail with age. Roots get into clay joints and cast iron rusts from inside, and a liner seals over both with jointless pipe that should outlast the original. Only one or two access points are needed.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Rapid Valley, SD

    Main water lines can also be replaced without a trench. We burst or bore. New HDPE or PEX goes in along the old path by bursting, or along a new one by directional boring, leaving lawns and drives alone. The line is pressure tested before service is turned back on.

    Same-Day Sewer Assessment in Rapid Valley, SD

    Sewer lines that fail suddenly can often be cleared and scoped the same day. Lining comes next. If a liner is a good fit, we book the repair quickly, keep the line flowing where possible in the meantime and tell you what to avoid flushing until the work is complete.

    Pipe Bursting in Rapid Valley, SD

    Bursting is used when the old pipe is too damaged to line or needs a larger diameter. It needs two pits. A bursting head is pulled through, fracturing the old pipe outward while a new HDPE pipe follows behind, and that HDPE is fused into one continuous length with no joints at all.

    Perma-Liner Trenchless Systems in Rapid Valley

    Perma-Liner is one of the CIPP systems our crews install. It's rated for sewer lines. The liners and resins are structural, meaning the cured pipe can stand on its own even if the old pipe around it keeps deteriorating, which is why we trust them for local lines. They handle harsh soil and flow.
