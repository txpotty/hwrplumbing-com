    Softener Not Regenerating in Rapid Valley

    Hard water coming back usually means the softener has stopped regenerating. A failed timer motor, a bad circuit board or a stuck meter turbine are the usual causes, along with settings lost in a power outage. Each part gets tested. Then we program the valve for your household and hardness, and soft water returns by the next cycle.

    Resin Testing and Replacement in Rapid Valley

    Resin beads trap hardness minerals and release them during regeneration. Chlorine and chloramine in city water slowly break resin down until it turns to mush. We test resin capacity, then clean it with resin cleaner or replace it, and a solid tank and valve with fresh resin works like new for years. Mushy resin can clog the valve.

    Hard Water Symptoms & Diagnostics in Rapid Valley

    Scale on faucets means trouble. HWR Plumbing checks hardness at the tap, inspects every component and gives you a repair plan for your Rapid Valley home. We test hardness before and after the repair, so you see the grains per gallon fall.

    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Rapid Valley, SD

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Sediment and salt residue clog its tiny ports. A clogged injector leaves the brine tank full of water and the resin never gets recharged. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. The bridge gets broken up and the tank cleaned out. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Fill the tank halfway.

    Control Valve Rebuild in Rapid Valley

    Service, backwash, brine and rinse are the four cycles the control valve directs every time the softener regenerates, usually in the middle of the night. Over the years, seals, spacers and pistons wear down. Common signs are leaks at the head or constant drain flow. A matching rebuild kit fixes it.
