    What Goes Wrong on a Disposal, and How We Fix It in Rapid Valley, SD

    Noisy Disposal Repair in Rapid Valley

    Grinding, rattling or a hum that sounds rough all point to something inside. A loose mount will rattle. We check the mount, then look for debris, then run the motor empty, since a whine with an empty chamber means the bearings are going.

    Leak Detection and Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Disposal leaks come from four places: the sink flange, the dishwasher inlet, the discharge tube and the housing itself. We dry everything, run water with a little food coloring and watch to see which joint shows color first, since water runs down the outside of the unit and hides the real source. Most leak points can be repaired. When the housing leaks, the unit has to go.

    Disposal Odor and Buildup Cleaning in Rapid Valley, SD

    Food buildup and grease on the chamber walls and under the splash guard cause most disposal smells. Hard water is the norm across much of South Dakota, and many groundwater sources carry dissolved minerals like calcium, magnesium, sulfate and iron. Scale gives that food something to stick to. We clean the splash guard and chamber, check the drain for buildup, and replace a worn guard, which often clears up a smell that months of lemon peels never touched.

    InSinkErator Disposal Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    The InSinkErator lineup runs from Badger units to the Evolution series, and we repair both. Badgers are simple single-stage units, while Evolution models have multi-stage grinding and, on some models, a separate stage that can jam on its own. Most repairs use parts from the truck. Motors on these units are not sold separately.

    InSinkErator and Other Brand Repairs in Rapid Valley, SD

    Every brand, from InSinkErator to Waste King and Moen, mounts differently, so a flange leak is fixed with that brand's kit. InSinkErator uses a three-bolt or Quick Lock style ring, while Waste King uses an EZ Mount twist ring that comes off without tools. Common kits ride on our trucks. It means most repairs need only one trip.

    Preventive Care and Longevity in Rapid Valley, SD

    Run cold water before, during and after grinding, and let it go about 15 seconds after the motor stops so the food clears the trap. Grease poured in with hot water coats the chamber and the pipe, while cold water keeps it solid so the impellers can break it up. Stringy peels cause most jams. Throw out celery, corn husks and artichoke leaves.

    Commercial Disposal Repair in Rapid Valley

    Restaurant disposals run 1 to 5 horsepower and often have a separate control panel and solenoid water valve. When one stops, the dish line backs up fast. Controls get checked first. A bad solenoid or tripped overload in the panel kills the unit as fully as a jam does, but it gets fixed far faster.

    Flange and Inlet Leak Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Leaks show up at the flange, dishwasher inlet, discharge tube or body. We trace the leak, reseal the flange with plumber's putty and replace gaskets or tubes as needed. Water from the bottom usually means a failed internal seal, which makes replacement the smarter move. It is not a repairable part.
