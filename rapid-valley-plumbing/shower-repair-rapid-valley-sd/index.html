    Shower Handle Leak Repair in Rapid Valley

    Leaks at the handle or behind the trim plate are easy to miss. Water running down the inside of the trim gets behind the tile at the valve opening, and over a few months it soaks the drywall around the valve without ever showing on the shower side. We pull the trim and look. Worn O-rings or a loose bonnet nut are the usual cause.

    Shower Pan and Wall Leak Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Stains on the ceiling below are often the first clue. We use a moisture meter on the walls and a flood test on the pan to tell a leaking valve from a cracked pan, a failed grout line or a loose drain body. Then we fix the source. On slab homes, these leaks can spread into the flooring of the next room before anyone sees them.

    Shower Drain Body Repair in Rapid Valley

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. When that happens, water goes under the shower instead of down the pipe, and the first sign is often a soft spot in the floor next to the shower or a stain in the ceiling below. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Dripping Showerhead Repair in Rapid Valley

    Drips from the head itself almost always come from the valve, not the head. Water that keeps coming out after the handle is off has to be getting past a worn cartridge or seat, so replacing the showerhead alone will not stop it. The fix is in the valve. Drips from the arm threads just need fresh tape and a snug turn.

    Weak Shower Spray Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Most weak showers come down to the head. Minerals fill the nozzles and the screen behind them, and the spray fades so gradually you barely notice until it is a mist. A soak can bring it back. A clogged cartridge gets replaced, then we test the flow.

    Mixing Valve Repair in Rapid Valley

    Mixing valves blend hot and cold inside the wall. A sticking cartridge or spool shows up as temperature that drifts or a handle that has to go much farther than it used to. A new cartridge usually fixes it. If the valve is too old for parts, we talk through a replacement valve and what it means for the tile.

    Moen and Delta Shower Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Moen 1222 and Delta RP46074 cartridges sit inside a huge share of the showers we open. Many of these carry a lifetime warranty to the original owner.
