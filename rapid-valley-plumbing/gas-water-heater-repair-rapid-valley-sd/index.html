    Pilot Light & Burner Issues in Rapid Valley

    A pilot that lights, holds for ten seconds and then goes out is almost always a thermocouple or a weak magnet in the gas valve. No flame at all usually means a plugged pilot orifice or a bad piezo. Burner trouble looks different. The pilot stays lit but the main flame never comes on, which points to the thermostat side of the valve, a tripped thermal cutoff or a flame arrestor so clogged with lint that the burner starves for air. We test each piece in that order.

    Thermostat and Gas Valve Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Scalding water at the kitchen tap is a valve problem until proven otherwise. Sensors can drift as they age, and dials sometimes get bumped past the hot mark. Our plumbers in Rapid Valley, SD measure the outlet temperature at the nearest fixture, compare it to the dial and replace the gas control if it is overshooting, because water at 140 degrees can burn a child's skin in a few seconds. A setting near 120 degrees works for most households.

    Tank Leaks & Anode Rods in Rapid Valley

    Corrosion starts at the anode rod and works its way into the steel once the rod is spent. The tech pulls the anode, checks what metal remains and swaps it if bare core wire shows. A fresh anode, a drained tank and a relief valve that seats properly can add years to a heater in a Rapid Valley home that has not started leaking from the shell. Water from the shell seam is another story. At that point the tank has to go.
