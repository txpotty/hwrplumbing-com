    Frequently Asked Questions About Shower Replacement in Rapid Valley

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. A new pan is usually $1,500 to $4,000, while a full tiled rebuild can reach $8,000 to $20,000. Valves are the cheapest fix.

    Kohler, Delta, Moen, American Standard and Sterling cover most of our installs in South Dakota homes. We can work with a brand you prefer or recommend options that fit your budget, with an eye on parts availability down the road. We care about future parts.

    For most homes, Moen or Delta valves are hard to beat. For the unit or the base, Sterling and American Standard make durable acrylic and fiberglass options, and Kohler makes plenty of both valves and bases. We fit it to your budget.

    Trim and showerheads are DIY-friendly. Valve, pan and drain replacements are hidden work where mistakes cause leaks, so a licensed plumber is the better choice.
