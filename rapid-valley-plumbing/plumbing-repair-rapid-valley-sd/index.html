    Water Softener Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Many rural homes in South Dakota draw from private wells or rural water systems, and a water test is the best way to know what comes out of the tap. White crust on faucets and spotty glasses are the first signs a softener has stopped working. The usual causes are a salt bridge in the brine tank or a plugged injector screen. HWR Plumbing repairs the common brands and tests the water hardness before and after the fix so you can see the number drop. Resin lasts ten to fifteen years.

    Slab Leak Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    A warm spot on tile, a meter that spins with every tap off, or a water bill that jumps for no reason. Those are slab leak signs. Acoustic listening gear and a thermal camera find the spot before anyone touches the concrete. Options include a spot repair through the slab or a reroute overhead, each priced separately.

    Sewer Pipe Repair in Rapid Valley, SD

    Clay and Orangeburg laterals crack and separate as the ground shifts, and roots find those gaps within a season or two. We camera the line and mark the depth and location of the problem on the surface. Repair options run from a traditional dig to pipe bursting or a cured-in-place liner. Every repair gets a final camera pass before the hole is closed.

    Gas Line Leak Repair in Rapid Valley

    Gas work is the one job no homeowner should try. Smell sulfur or hear hissing, and the right move is to walk out and call from the driveway.     Water Leak Repair in Rapid Valley

    Hidden leaks sit inside walls, under sink cabinets and in the buried yard line that runs between the meter and the house. A thermal camera narrows it down.     Tank and Tankless Water Heater Repair in Rapid Valley

    Tank and tankless heaters fail in different ways. HWR Plumbing repairs both, across all the major brands, gas and electric. Tank calls usually come down to the thermocouple, the gas valve, an element or the anode. Tankless trouble is usually scale in the heat exchanger or a dirty inlet screen, and a descaling flush fixes more of them than new parts do.
