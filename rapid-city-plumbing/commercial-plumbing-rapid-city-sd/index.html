    What Our Commercial Plumbers Do in Rapid City, SD

    Commercial Leak Detection in Rapid City, SD

    Water bills that jump 30 percent with no change in business usually mean a hidden leak or a stuck fixture. Start with the meter. We shut fixtures off zone by zone and watch the meter, then use acoustic sensors, thermal cameras and tracer gas to find leaks in walls, ceilings and buried service lines. Sometimes the culprit is just a running toilet. A single stuck flush valve can burn through thousands of gallons in a week.

    Commercial Pipe Repair and Repiping in Rapid City, SD

    Older commercial buildings often have galvanized supply lines that are rusted nearly shut, or copper that has pinholed along hot water runs. Patching works for a while. Once leaks start showing up in different spots every few months, a sectional repipe in Type L copper or commercial-grade PEX usually costs less than the next three emergency calls. The first step is mapping the risers and branches. Work gets phased by floor or suite so tenants keep their water most of the day.

    Commercial Drain Cleaning and Hydro-Jetting in Rapid City

    Commercial drains carry grease, paper towels and food waste in volumes no house ever sees. A cable just pokes a hole. We use high-pressure jetting with rotating nozzles that scrub grease off the whole circumference of 4-inch and 6-inch lines, not just the bottom. Jetting keeps the line open for months instead of days. Restaurants, schools and apartment stacks are where we see the biggest difference.

    Commercial Restroom Plumbing in Rapid City

    A broken flushometer in a busy restroom gets noticed by every customer who walks in. We repair and replace Sloan and Zurn flush valves, sensor faucets, wall-hung toilets and carriers. We also handle ADA upgrades. That includes setting fixtures at the right heights, adding blocking for grab bars and wrapping exposed drain lines under lavatories.

    Kitchen and Floor Drain Cleaning in Rapid City

    Floor drains in commercial kitchens collect grease, rice, mop water and the odd utensil. Most kitchens see the same drain back up every few months. We open the line with a sectional cable, then jet the 2-inch and 3-inch branches so the grease does not just close back up in a week. Trap primers get checked too.

    Sewer Camera Inspection for Businesses in Rapid City

    A camera inspection shows the inside of a drain line on a monitor and records it so you can share it with a landlord or contractor. Guesswork goes away. We push the camera from a cleanout, note the footage at every break, root mass or sag, and use a locator to mark depth and position on the ground above. The video is yours to keep. That footage helps you budget repairs and explain them to owners or a board.

    Commercial Plumbing Costs and Maintenance Plans in Rapid City, SD

    Backflow tests often run $75 to $175 per assembly, and kitchen line jetting usually costs $350 to $900 depending on length and buildup. After-hours calls cost more. Putting testing, jetting and flushing on a plan evens out your costs and cuts down on emergency calls, which cost the most. Every plan comes as a written proposal.

    Grease Trap Installation in Rapid City

    A new restaurant or a kitchen expansion usually needs a grease interceptor sized from the fixtures that drain to it. Small kitchens can get by with an under-sink trap, but bigger operations often need an in-ground interceptor of 500 gallons or more. Sizing is set by code. We size the unit from the equipment schedule, set it at the right grade and vent it properly so it passes inspection the first time.
