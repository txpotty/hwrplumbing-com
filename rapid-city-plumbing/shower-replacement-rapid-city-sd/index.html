    Old Shower Valve Replacement in Rapid City

    Two- and three-handle valves from decades back often leak and lack scald protection. We open the wall from the back side when we can, remove the old valve and install a pressure-balance or thermostatic valve, then cover the old opening with an oversized trim plate. Usually no new tile. It is a big safety upgrade.

    Walk-In Shower Conversion in Rapid City, SD

    Walk-in conversions are one of the most requested bathroom jobs we do. The drain moves to the pan's center, the valve goes up to shower height and the slope keeps water from escaping the entry. Grab bar blocking is easy now. ADA layouts are available.

    Custom Tile Shower Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    Custom tile replacement starts with careful demolition. We take the old tile and pan down to the studs and subfloor, replace any damaged wood and install a new waterproofed pan and walls, then set the valve and drain to the finished tile depth. Slope matters most. A flood test comes before tile.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Rapid City

    Old valves waste water and swing temperature. We replace corroded valves with modern pressure-balancing or thermostatic models, usually working through an access panel on the back side of the wall so the tile stays in place. Larger trim plates cover old openings. It works alone or as part of a rebuild.

    Commercial Shower Replacement in Rapid City

    Gyms and hotels need showers that survive heavy use. Commercial replacements get heavy-duty valves, tamper-resistant trim and properly sized drains to code. We phase the job. That way some showers stay open while others are replaced.

    Replacing Showers Damaged by Hard Water in Rapid City, SD

    Hard water is the norm across much of South Dakota, and many groundwater sources carry dissolved minerals like calcium, magnesium, sulfate and iron. Scale shows up first as a weak showerhead and a stiff handle. Over enough years, it can lock a cartridge in place, pit a valve body and etch glass doors past the point where cleaning helps, and that is when replacement makes sense. Softened water protects the new shower. Ask us about options.

    Shower Replacement Options and Costs in Rapid City, SD

    Showerhead and Trim Upgrade in Rapid City, SD

    If the valve is sound, new trim and a new head can refresh the shower without opening the wall. We match a trim kit to your existing valve brand, install a new head or handheld and add a slide bar if you want one. Rubber nozzles wipe clean. It costs the least.

    Shower valve replacement usually costs $450 to $1,200. Shower pan replacement generally runs $1,500 to $4,000, and a full rebuild with tile can cost $8,000 to $20,000 or more depending on finishes and repairs. Valves are the cheapest fix.
