    Well Pump Brands We Install in Rapid City, SD

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Sta-Rite cover nearly every well pump we install. Each brand has strengths, and we choose based on your well. Goulds and Franklin parts are on every supply shelf, which helps when a control box fails late on a Saturday afternoon.

    Solar Well Pump Systems in Rapid City, SD

    Solar well pumps make sense for livestock tanks and cabins where running power from the meter would cost more than the pump itself. A few panels feed a DC pump through a controller while the sun is up. The storage tank does the job a battery would. We size panels to your daily gallons.

    Franklin Electric Well Pump Replacement in Rapid City

    Franklin Electric builds a large share of the submersible motors sold in the US, including ones sitting under other brands' pump ends. Replacement control boxes are easy to source. We match the motor to the pump end and the wire size to the run length.

    Grundfos Well Pump Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have a built-in soft start and dry-run protection, and they run on two wires with no control box at the surface. They cost more up front. Add the CU 301 controller to an SQE and you get constant pressure.

    Well Pump Sizing in Rapid City

    Sizing starts with the well log. We get depth and static level from the well log or a sounder, then a draw-down test shows what the well can really produce in gallons per minute. Pump more than the well yields and it runs dry.

    Drop Pipe and Wire Replacement in Rapid City

    Galvanized drop pipe from the 1970s and 1980s rusts from the inside, and a pinhole at 150 feet will bleed pressure every time the pump shuts off. We replace it with schedule 120 PVC or poly pipe, depending on the depth and the pump weight. The wire gets checked too. Nicked jackets and old tape splices go.

    Jet Pump Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    Jet pumps sit above ground, in a well house or basement, and pull water up by suction from wells where the water level is within about 25 feet. Deep-well jets with a two-pipe setup can reach further, but they work harder. The common failure is losing prime. Usually a leaking foot valve or a cracked suction line lets the water fall back down the well, so we check both before we blame the pump.

    No-Water Calls in Rapid City

    Pump trouble rarely gives notice. Usually the faucet spits air, then quits, after the motor has been limping along for weeks. Before we pull the pump, we test the switch and control box.
