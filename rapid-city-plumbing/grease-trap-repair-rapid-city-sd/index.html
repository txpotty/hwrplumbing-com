    Commercial Grease Interceptor Repair in Rapid City, SD

    In-ground gravity interceptors run from about 500 gallons to several thousand, usually precast concrete with two compartments.     Flow Control Device Repair in Rapid City

    Hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, like 20 or 50 gallons per minute, and the flow control fitting is what holds the water to that rate. Take it out and a three-compartment sink dump blows grease right past the baffles. Inspectors look for it. We also clear the air intake, since a plugged intake on the flow control fitting lets the trap siphon.

    Grease Trap Leak Repair in Rapid City

    Plastic and fiberglass trap bodies do not rust, but they can crack from a dropped lid or a dishwasher dumping water hotter than the trap is rated for. Hairline cracks sometimes take a plastic weld. Anything through the floor of the tank or along a molded seam we treat as a replacement, because patches there fail under the weight of a full trap. We will show you the crack before recommending anything.

    Damaged Baffle and Lid Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Lids crack when someone sets a stockpot on them or a dolly rolls across a floor-mounted unit. Once cracked, it leaks smell out and lets mop water into the trap. We replace the lid and gasket with the manufacturer part, and on in-floor units we check that the lid sits flush so nobody catches a toe or a cart wheel on it. Baffle damage often shows up at the same visit.

    Inlet and Outlet Tee Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Tees are cheap parts with an outsized job. On the inlet side, the tee directs incoming water down under the floating grease. The outlet tee draws water from low in the tank, well under the grease cap, so if it breaks off or someone removes it during pumping, floating grease walks right out to the sewer. New tees go in as Schedule 40 PVC, cut to the drop length the manufacturer or local code specifies.

    Grease Interceptor Clog Removal in Rapid City

    Grease does not stay liquid for long once it cools in a pipe. Within a few feet of the sink it hardens like candle wax along the top of the pipe. Chemical drain openers soften it for a day and then it re-forms farther downstream, sometimes past the trap entirely, where it becomes a sewer problem instead of a kitchen problem. A jetter actually takes it out of the line.

    Hydro-Jetting for Grease Trap Repair in Rapid City

    A cable machine punches a hole through grease. A jetter scrubs the whole pipe wall instead. We run a commercial jetter at 3,000 to 4,000 PSI through the line feeding the trap and the line leaving it, working from the cleanout toward the main. With old cast iron, we turn the pressure down and keep a camera on it, since a thin wall can blow out.
