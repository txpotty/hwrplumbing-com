    Residential Frozen Pipe Thawing in Rapid City

    A faucet that trickles or goes dry on a cold morning usually means ice in the line feeding it. We trace the line back with a thermal camera to find the cold spot. Once we know where it is, we thaw it with controlled heat while the faucet stays open, so the melting water has a way out and pressure cannot build behind the ice.

    Preventive Pipe Winterization in Rapid City

    Winterizing before the first hard freeze is cheaper than one burst pipe. We walk the house and mark every exposed run. We insulate attic lines, cover vents, upgrade outdoor faucets and drain irrigation lines.

    Frozen Pipe Thawing in Rapid City

    When a faucet only trickles or nothing comes out after a cold night, ice is blocking the line. First we open the faucet and locate the ice. Copper and steel can be thawed with an electric machine in minutes, while PEX and CPVC get infrared or warm air, which takes longer but is safe for plastic. Patience pays here.

    Thawing Drain Lines and Sewer Pipes in Rapid City

    Drain lines can freeze too, usually where a line runs through an unheated crawlspace or an exterior wall. You will see the backup first. Hot water through the cleanout thaws it, and then we check for a sag that holds water.

    Urgent Frozen Pipe Thawing in Rapid City

    Time matters once a line freezes, because the longer ice sits, the more pressure builds behind it. Open the faucet on that line now. Most frozen lines flow again within an hour of arrival, and every one gets a pressure test.
