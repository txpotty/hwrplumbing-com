    Injector and Brine Line Cleaning in Rapid City, SD

    The injector, sometimes called a venturi, creates the suction that draws brine into the resin tank. Its small ports clog with sediment and salt. Once it clogs, the brine tank fills with water instead of emptying, and the softener regenerates with plain water that does nothing to the resin. We clean or replace it and check the brine line for air leaks.

    Bypass and Fitting Leak Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Bypass valves, the O-rings where the valve meets the tank, cracked fittings and the drain line are the usual leak points. Even a small drip wastes water and can stain a garage floor or rot a cabinet. We replace O-rings or fittings and test under pressure. Mineral tank cracks cannot be fixed.

    Brine Tank and Salt Bridge Repair in Rapid City, SD

    A crust of salt across the brine tank stops regeneration, and hard water shows up at every tap within a day or two. We break up and remove the bridge and clean the tank. Cracked brine tanks on older units get replaced. Keep the salt about half full.

    No-Salt Water Conditioner Repair in Rapid City, SD

    No-salt conditioners do not remove minerals, they change how scale forms, but they still need service. Scale returning or a drop in water pressure usually means the conditioning media is spent or the sediment prefilter is clogged. HWR Plumbing repairs these units in Rapid City, SD. The media wears out in a few years.

    Resin Bed Replacement in Rapid City

    Chlorine eats resin slowly, and after enough years the beads swell, break and stop exchanging the calcium and magnesium they are supposed to hold. Older systems on chlorinated city water see it most. HWR Plumbing removes the old resin and installs fresh, high-capacity media. The softener works like new.
