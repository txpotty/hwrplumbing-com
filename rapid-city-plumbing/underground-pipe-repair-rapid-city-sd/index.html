    Pipe Bursting Services in Rapid City, SD

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Galvanized and Polybutylene Line Replacement in Rapid City

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene splits instead. If your buried service is either one, patches are usually temporary, so we replace the line with PEX or Type K copper, often pulling the new pipe through the old path to limit digging. You will notice the pressure.
