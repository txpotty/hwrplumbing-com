    PEX and HDPE Water Line Installation in Rapid City, SD

    HDPE and PEX both flex, so there are fewer fittings in the ground and better tolerance for soil movement.     PEX Water Line Installation in Rapid City

    Cross-linked polyethylene, or PEX, bends around corners and tolerates a little ground movement. Long, cold winters and hot summers put South Dakota plumbing through wide temperature swings that expand and contract pipes and fittings every year. It goes in on a sand bed with tracer wire run next to it, because PEX is plastic and a locator cannot find it later without that wire on top of the pipe in Rapid City. UV light weakens it.
