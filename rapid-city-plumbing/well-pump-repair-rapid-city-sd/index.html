    Grundfos SQ and SQE pumps have built-in electronics that protect the motor and report faults. Sensor failures and drive errors are the usual problems. HWR Plumbing reads the fault code, checks the wiring and tests the pressure sensor before replacing anything in a Rapid City, SD well. Usually the electronics are fine.

    Well Pump Brands We Service in Rapid City, SD

    Goulds, Grundfos, Franklin Electric and Myers make most of the pumps we pull in this area, and HWR Plumbing services all of them.     Pressure Switch Replacement in Rapid City

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. Pitted contacts, a clogged sensing tube and ant nests are the usual reasons one fails. After swapping the switch and clearing the tube, we set cut-in and cut-out to match the tank, usually 40 and 60 psi. One of the cheaper well repairs.

    Jet Pump Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Shallow wells and booster systems often use jet pumps. Lost prime, seal leaks and impeller wear account for most jet pump calls. Our crew in Rapid City restores prime, replaces worn parts and checks the suction line for air leaks. Where water levels swing through the year, a falling level can starve the pump and cost it prime.

    Control Box and Surge Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Three-wire submersible pumps rely on a control box with a start capacitor and a relay. Summer thunderstorms can drop a lot of rain in a short time, pushing water back through floor drains when sewer lines are overloaded. Lightning and power surges damage those parts more than anything else. After testing the capacitor, relay and overload, we replace the bad part and suggest a surge arrestor if the box lacks one. An arrestor costs little.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Rapid City, SD

    Jet pumps sit above ground and pull water up through a suction line with an ejector. Most failures start with an air leak somewhere in the suction line. HWR Plumbing checks the foot valve, the ejector nozzle and every suction fitting, then reprimes the pump and confirms it builds pressure. Nonstop running without pressure usually means lost prime or an air leak.
