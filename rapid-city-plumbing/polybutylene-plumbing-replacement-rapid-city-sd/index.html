    Manufactured Home Poly Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    Manufactured homes from the poly era often have polybutylene lines in the floor or the belly underneath. New PEX gets sized for the home and strapped to the frame. Exposed lines get checked. Frozen pipes are a real and regular risk in South Dakota, with deep frost each winter that can reach water service lines buried at shallow depth.     Why Polybutylene Fails in Rapid City

    Polybutylene reacts with oxidizers like chlorine that water utilities use to disinfect tap water. The inner wall of the pipe and fittings slowly turns brittle, and small cracks grow until the pipe splits. Mineral scale builds up quickly in water heaters, faucet aerators and fixtures here, so a water softener is a common upgrade for homeowners. Hot water speeds it up. Most of the failures we see are on hot lines and at fittings close to the heater.

    Emergency Polybutylene Pipe Repair in Rapid City, SD

    A burst poly fitting can push water across a floor or through a ceiling in minutes. Shut the main first. Our plumber stops the leak, replaces the failed section or fitting with a temporary repair that gets water back on and then schedules the full replacement so you are not rushed into a decision. Photos of the failure help with your insurance claim.

    Copper Replacement for Polybutylene in Rapid City, SD

    Some homeowners in Rapid City, SD want copper when they take out polybutylene. That works fine. Our copper replacements use thicker Type L pipe with lead-free soldered or pressed joints, not the thinner Type M. Copper costs more and takes longer, since each turn needs a fitting, but it tolerates heat and sunlight well and has a long track record.

    PEX Pipe Installation in Rapid City, SD

    PEX is the most common replacement for polybutylene because it is flexible, resists scale and corrosion and installs faster than copper. Labor runs lower with PEX. Attic PEX stays out of UV, clear of flues and can lights and supported as the maker specifies, which protects the pipe and the warranty.

    Copper Pipe Replacement in Rapid City

    Copper is a solid alternative to PEX for poly replacement. It is rigid, handles heat well and has decades of history in homes. Plan on a higher price. Some water can pinhole copper over time, so we look at your water and any past leak history before recommending it over PEX.

    Fitting Failure and Emergency Repair in Rapid City

    Acetal plastic and copper crimp ring fittings are often the first part of a poly system to fail. Attic fittings fail hard. We respond to emergency leaks, make temporary repairs and then schedule a full replacement, because stopping the problem for good means taking the poly system out of service entirely.
