    Brine Tank Cleanout and Inspection in Rapid City, SD

    Brine tanks collect a gray sludge of salt fines and insoluble grit, and it builds up faster with cheap rock salt. The tank gets emptied, scrubbed and rinsed. Then we look for cracks near the overflow elbow and the brine well and refill with fresh salt.

    Yearly Water Softener Tune-Ups in Rapid City

    A tune-up starts with two hardness readings and ends with a full manual regeneration that we watch from start to finish. Between those, the salt level, brine well and float all get a look. Figure on an hour for a typical unit.

    Control Valve Diagnostics and Repair in Rapid City

    Leaks at the valve body, water running to drain around the clock, a motor that hums without moving or a display stuck on one cycle usually point to worn seals and spacers. On a Fleck 5600 the fix is a seal kit and a new piston, often done in under an hour without pulling the valve off the tank. Cheaper than a new head.

    Bypass Valve and Drain Line Repairs in Rapid City, SD

    A bypass valve that does not seat fully lets raw water blend into the softened supply, and the hardness test is usually the first clue. The bypass gets rebuilt or swapped out and check the drain line for kinks. The drain line has to end above its receptor with an air gap.

    Breaking Up Salt Bridges in Rapid City

    To check for a bridge, we push a broom handle straight down through the salt. If it hits a hard layer and then falls through, there is a bridge. We break the crust, pull out the mushy salt underneath with a wet-dry vac, then check the brine well, safety float, brine line and grid plate before refilling to about half full.

    Commercial Softener Service in Rapid City

    Commercial units work harder. Restaurants and laundromats can burn through a softener's capacity in a single day, so twin-tank systems swap tanks, keeping one in service while the other regenerates. We set a service schedule around your hours so the water never goes hard during a rush.

    Salt-Free Conditioner Service in Rapid City

    Salt-free conditioners, usually template assisted crystallization units, do not remove hardness. A hardness test still reads high afterward. They turn the hardness into microscopic crystals that mostly pass through without sticking. Plan on new media every three to five years, and the sediment prefilter ahead of it needs changing every few months, which is the part most owners skip.
