    Pressure Switch and Gauge Integration in Rapid City, SD

    The pressure switch tells the pump when to start and stop. It works with the tank, not apart from it. A switch set at 40/60 needs a tank pre-charged to 38 psi, and a gauge that reads correctly so you can see what the system is doing, so we replace a worn gauge rather than trusting a sticky needle. Small parts, big role.

    Well-Rite Pressure Tank Installation in Rapid City, SD

    Well-Rite tanks use a replaceable diaphragm design in many models. We install them in the common residential sizes. Like any tank, the key is setting pre-charge before it fills, and checking that drawdown matches the pump. A good tank installed badly fails early.

    Constant Pressure System Conversion in Rapid City, SD

    Constant pressure systems use a variable speed drive that adjusts pump speed to demand. Pressure stays steady even with two showers running. These systems can run on a smaller tank, but they cost more up front and need a compatible pump and drive. We explain the trade-off.

    Emergency Pressure Tank Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    If the pump is running nonstop or the taps are dry, call (605) 480-9736. A ruptured tank or a stuck switch can burn out a pump, so we isolate the system, diagnose it and swap the tank, often the same day. Turn off the breaker first.

    Diaphragm Pressure Tank Replacement in Rapid City, SD

    Diaphragm tanks use a flexible sheet clamped inside the shell. Air sits above it, water below. When it fails, symptoms match a bladder tank, including fast cycling and water at the Schrader valve. Replacement is the usual answer.

    Pressure Tank Sizing and Upsizing in Rapid City

    Drawdown is the usable water between cut-out and cut-in. Small tanks give surprisingly little. We divide pump flow by drawdown to see how many starts per hour the pump will face, and target a minimum one-minute run time. That often points to a bigger tank.

    Large Capacity Tanks for Ranch and Commercial Wells in Rapid City

    Big properties need big drawdown. Livestock and irrigation draw a lot. We install large capacity tanks or banks of tanks on a manifold, sizing them so the pump runs long enough each cycle even under heavy demand. Floor space matters.

    Amtrol Pressure Tank Replacement in Rapid City

    Replacing an Amtrol tank with another Amtrol is often straightforward. The connection height and size usually match. Even so, we confirm the model is still the right size for your pump, since many original tanks were undersized from the start. Check before swapping.
