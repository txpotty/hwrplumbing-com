    Shower Drain Body Repair in Yankton

    Drain bodies can crack or work loose under the pan. A loose or cracked drain lets water go under the pan, which shows up as a soft floor beside the shower or a ceiling stain. We clear and test the drain. We reseal or replace the drain body as needed.

    Showerhead and Arm Leak Repair in Yankton

    Shower arms leak at the threads more than people realize. A leak at the arm threads follows the pipe into the wall, and it shows up as bubbling paint in the next room. We reseal the threads. We can add a handheld or slide bar while we are there.

    Low Shower Pressure Repair in Yankton, SD

    Low pressure at the shower with good pressure at the sink narrows it down fast. With good pressure elsewhere, the problem is in the head, the restrictor or the cartridge, and that is typically a short repair. The head comes off first. Low pressure everywhere sends us to the pressure regulator and main valve instead.

    Shower Valve Replacement in Yankton

    Temperature that swings or a handle that fights you points to the valve.     Shower Temperature Swing Repair in Yankton

    Cold blasts when a toilet flushes mean the pressure-balance spool is stuck. The spool should shift to keep the mix steady when one side loses pressure, but scale can lock it in one spot.     Tub and Shower Combo Repair in Yankton, SD
