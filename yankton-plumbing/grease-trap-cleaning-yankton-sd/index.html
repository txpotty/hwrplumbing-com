    Under-Sink Grease Trap Maintenance in Yankton, SD

    Under-sink hydromechanical traps are rated by flow, usually 10 to 50 gallons per minute, and they hold far less than people expect. In a busy kitchen, that means cleaning every week or two. The service covers grease and solids removal, a baffle scrape, a look at the inlet flow control fitting, and a new lid gasket if the old one is flattened.     Grease Trap Hydro-Jetting in Yankton, SD

    Some traps have years of grease baked onto the walls and inlet piping. That crust survives a normal pump-out. We jet the inside of the empty trap and the line feeding it, which takes the unit back to bare walls and restores the capacity the crust was eating up. It adds about an hour.

    Large Outdoor Grease Interceptor Cleaning in Yankton

    Gravity interceptors in the parking lot often hold 750 to 2,000 gallons across two or three compartments. We pump each compartment on its own. No one goes inside without confined-space gear.
