    Shower Pan Installation in Mitchell

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. Prefab pans get a level check. Both kinds get slope toward the drain.

    Tub-to-Shower Conversion in Mitchell, SD

    Tub-to-shower conversions are one of our most common jobs. The tub comes out, the drain shifts to the pan location and the valve moves up to about 48 inches. On a slab, we cut concrete. No more stepping over a tub wall.

    Curbless Shower Installation in Mitchell

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. On a wood floor, we drop the joists or use a recessed pan system, and on a slab, we cut and lower the concrete so the finished floor runs flat into the shower. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Emergency Shower System Replacement in Mitchell, SD

    Water under a failed pan can rot a wood subfloor in a season. A cracked pan or a leaking valve means pulling the shower, checking the framing and getting a new unit in as soon as it is safe. The framing must be dry. Then the new shower goes in.

    Tiled Walk-In Shower Installation in Mitchell

    Frameless glass walk-ins are popular, and the plumbing behind them has to be dead on. We install the valve, the head, an optional handheld and the drain, then set the mortar bed or foam pan to slope toward the drain at a quarter inch per foot. Then we test. The valve gets a pressure test and the pan a 24-hour flood test before tile.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Mitchell, SD

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. We set the base on mortar, connect the drain, install the valve and trim and make sure the walls are fastened so nothing flexes when someone leans on them. Good for rentals and guest baths. Usually a one-day job.

    Walk-In Shower Installation in Mitchell

    Walk-in showers depend on floor slope. Without a curb to hold water back, the floor has to pitch a quarter inch per foot toward the drain, and the waterproofing has to run well past the shower opening. We set that slope. We add blocking for grab bars while the studs are bare.

    Budget Shower Upgrades in Mitchell, SD

    Budget showers can still be built right. A fiberglass or acrylic unit with a quality pressure-balance valve costs far less than a tiled shower, and when it is set on a mortar bed with a sealed drain, it lasts decades. Spend on the valve. You get the full price before we start.
