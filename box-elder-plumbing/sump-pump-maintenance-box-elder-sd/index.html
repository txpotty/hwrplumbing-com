    Battery Backup Sump Pump Maintenance in Box Elder, SD

    Backup pumps sit in the pit next to the main pump and run off a 12-volt battery when the power drops or the primary fails. We check the battery with a load tester, not just a voltmeter. Then we unplug the main pump and let the backup empty the pit on its own. Alarms get tested too.

    Zoeller Sump Pump Service in Box Elder

    Zoeller pumps like the M53 and M63 use cast iron housings and an integral float switch, and they tend to outlast cheaper plastic units. Even so, we look for cracks in the casting and make sure the float moves freely. Common Zoeller parts ride on our trucks.

    Commercial Sump Pump Maintenance in Box Elder, SD

    Commercial pits are often bigger and deeper, and many have two pumps on an alternating controller. Each pump gets tested, along with the alternator and the high-water alarm. You get a written record of each reading, which comes in handy for insurance renewals and inspections. Quarterly visits are common.

    Sump Pit Cleaning and Debris Removal in Box Elder

    Silt and gravel wash into a pit through the drain tile every time it rains, and over a few years that layer can bury the intake screen. Our plumber drains the basin and digs the sediment out by hand. A clean pit lets the float move freely. The pump runs cooler too.

    Float Switch Checks in Box Elder, SD

    The float switch tells the pump when to start and stop, and a float with too little travel between on and off makes the pump short cycle dozens of times an hour. We watch the float through a full cycle. Then we set the on and off points so the pump runs longer, less often.

    Backup Pump Battery Service in Box Elder

    Outages and heavy rain tend to arrive together. The backup takes over when the primary loses power or simply stops. Our plumber load tests the battery, tests the charger and alarm, then runs the backup alone with the primary unplugged. Expect a new battery every three to five years.

    Wayne Sump Pump Service in Box Elder, SD

    Some Wayne models draw water through a top suction intake, which helps them avoid pulling in the sludge that settles at the bottom of a pit. That intake gets cleaned, and so does the float mechanism. Loose discharge fittings show up on every brand. Easy fix.

    Float Switch Repair and Adjustment in Box Elder, SD

    Float trouble causes more no-start calls than anything else. Tethered floats can wrap around the discharge pipe or snag on the pit wall. Vertical floats stick when mineral crust builds up on the rod. In tight pits where nothing has room to swing, we can switch you to an electronic sensor switch with no moving float at all.
