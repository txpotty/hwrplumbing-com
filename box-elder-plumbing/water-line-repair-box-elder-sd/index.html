    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Box Elder

    A trench through a paver driveway or a mature garden can cost more to restore than the pipe itself. That is where trenchless pays off. We dig a pit at each end and pull HDPE or PEX through with a boring tool or pipe puller, following the old line or cutting a new path beside it. Most replacements finish in a day. The new pipe carries its own manufacturer warranty.

    Copper Water Line Repair in Box Elder

    Buried copper is tough, but it is not forever. Rocks pressing against the pipe wear pinholes over time, and acidic soil or high water velocity can thin the wall from the inside. We usually cut out two feet. The new piece goes in with soldered or pressed couplings, and we check the exposed pipe on both sides for green crust or soft spots.

    Underground Leak Detection in Box Elder, SD

    A ground microphone picks up the hiss of water escaping a pressurized pipe, even through a couple of feet of dirt. Quiet leaks are harder. For those we push a hydrogen and nitrogen tracer gas into the drained line and sniff for it at the surface, a method that catches leaks too quiet for acoustic gear. Soils in South Dakota range from glacial deposits in the east to clay rich ground farther west that swells when wet and shrinks when dry. Once the spot is marked, you get a written repair price before we dig.

    Emergency Water Line Repair in Box Elder, SD

    First thing on a burst line is shutting the water off, at the house valve if it works and at the curb stop if it does not. Free-running water washes soil out from under walks and slabs fast. HWR Plumbing sends a plumber with a curb key, a pump and repair couplings. We can usually stop it within the hour. If the repair will run long, we rig a temporary bypass so the house has water while we work.

    Emergency Water Line Break Repair in Box Elder, SD

    A full break can flood the yard and leave the house dry at the same time. Shutting the water off comes first. The crew pumps the hole, finds the break and decides whether a coupling will do or whether a section needs replacing, and if the repair will run past a few hours we set a temporary bypass to the house. Most breaks are fixed the same day. We photograph the damage for your insurance or HOA.

    Pressure Regulator and Shutoff Repair in Box Elder

    High static pressure shortens the life of the service line and every fixture behind it. The usual code limit is 80 psi. We gauge the pressure at a hose bib, then swap a failed regulator for a new Watts or Zurn Wilkins valve when it reads too high. Old gate valves that will not close get replaced with full-port ball valves. So the shutoff works when you need it.

    Slab Leak Water Line Repair in Box Elder

    A slab leak is a supply pipe leaking under the concrete floor of the house. Older homes in Box Elder may still have galvanized supply lines that narrow with rust and scale, causing low water pressure and discolored water. Signs include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water and a meter that moves with everything off. We pinpoint the leak with acoustic gear and thermal imaging. From there you have three choices: break the slab at the spot, reroute the line through the walls or attic, or abandon the old pipe under the floor and run new PEX above it. You get a price on every option.

    Water Pressure Restoration in Box Elder

    Low pressure at every fixture points to the service line or the regulator, not the faucets. We put a gauge on a hose bib first. Then we check the pressure reducing valve, the main shutoff and the curb valve for anything half closed, and look at the pipe itself. Old galvanized can be choked to half its size with rust. The fix might be a new valve or a new line.
