    Pipe Bursting Services in Box Elder, SD

    Pipe bursting pulls a new pipe through the old one while breaking the old pipe outward. New pipe follows the old path. A bursting head on a cable splits clay, cast iron or brittle plastic, and fused HDPE follows right behind it, often at the same size or one size larger than the original. It works on badly damaged lines. Lining cannot do that.

    Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until little water gets through. Polybutylene cracks without warning.
