    What a Plumbing Inspection Covers in Box Elder, SD

    Sewer Camera Inspection in Box Elder, SD

    Many older homes have clay, cast iron or Orangeburg sewer lines that can crack, sag or fill with roots. We run a high-resolution camera from a cleanout to the city connection or septic tank. The video shows offsets, bellies, roots and breaks along with their depth and location, and you keep a copy of the recording for your records or for a contractor.

    Hard Water Damage Assessment in Box Elder

    Many rural homes in South Dakota draw from private wells or rural water systems, and a water test is the best way to know what comes out of the tap. Hard water leaves scale in pipes, fixtures and water heaters. We look for crusty aerators, scaled showerheads, slow-filling toilets and sediment in the water heater, and we can run a simple hardness test on the spot. You get options, not a pitch. Softeners, filters and scale reducers all help, and we suggest the one that matches the reading we took.

    Sewer Line Camera Inspections in Box Elder, SD

    A sewer camera shows the inside of the main drain without digging. We push the camera in from a cleanout, watch for cracks, roots, offsets and low spots and record the whole run to the street or septic tank. A locator marks the depth. Shifting clay and deep frost can stress buried sewer and water lines, so a camera inspection is useful when drains back up repeatedly.

    Gas Connection Safety Check in Box Elder, SD

    During a full inspection, we look at the visible gas piping and the connectors to each appliance. Old brass connectors, missing shutoffs or drip legs and rust all get noted, and we sweep joints with a gas detector. Any leak is reported on the spot. Most of our gas findings are in older homes, often at original appliance connectors.

    Water Heater and Softener Inspection in Box Elder

    We check the water heater's age by serial number, look for rust, leaks and sediment and test the relief valve. Venting and gas connections get checked. We also look at the expansion tank, drain pan and softener, and a hardness test shows whether the softener is working or just eating salt.

    Fixture, Drain and Shutoff Valve Check in Box Elder, SD

    We run every faucet, shower and toilet and look for slow drains, leaks and weak flow. Shutoffs get tested too. Angle stops and the main shutoff valve often seize over the years, and we also listen for gurgling drains that point to venting problems, since small fixes like these are easy to plan once you know about them.

    Whole-Home Plumbing Inspections in Box Elder

    Our whole-home inspections focus on the problems we see most in South Dakota houses. Common finds include old pipe, stiff shutoffs, tired heaters, slow drains and rooty or sagging sewers. We test each one directly. A corroded fitting or partial blockage found now will not become a ceiling stain or a holiday backup later.
