    Kinds of Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Commercial Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Commercial restrooms take a beating, so we install vitreous china bowls with flushometer valves rather than tank toilets. A flushometer needs a 1 inch supply and steady pressure. Before setting fixtures in an office or shop, we check that the building supply can deliver the flow the valve is rated for, often 20 to 25 gallons per minute at the fixture. Fixture counts follow code.

    Toilet Brands We Install in Sioux Falls

    We install Kohler, TOTO, American Standard, Gerber and most other brands sold at supply houses and home centers. Each one has its own parts quirks. Some skirted TOTO models use a Unifit adapter, and certain Kohler tanks take a gasket that hardware stores rarely stock. We keep those parts on hand.

    Standard Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls

    A standard floor-mount toilet is a two-hour job when the flange is sound. The stop gets shut, the tank drained, and the old bowl lifted out. Once the old wax is scraped off, the flange gets checked for cracks, new stainless bolts go in, and the new bowl is lowered straight onto a fresh seal. The old one leaves with us.

    Rough-In Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Rough-in is the plumbing that goes in before walls and floors close up. On a toilet, that is a vented 3 inch drain and a cold water stub. We set the drain 12 inches from the framed wall plus the thickness of drywall, so the finished rough-in lands right where the fixture expects it. Then the inspector sees it open.

    Wall-Hung Carrier Systems in Sioux Falls

    In-wall carriers from Geberit and Grohe each mount a little differently, and the instructions matter. The frame height chart from the maker gets followed to the letter. Set the frame too low and the bowl ends up at child height, too high and nobody's feet touch the floor, and neither can be fixed once the wall is tiled. Measure twice here.

    Commercial Flushometer Toilets in Sioux Falls

    Sloan and Zurn flushometer valves are the standard on commercial toilets for a reason. They recover instantly between flushes. We install manual and sensor-operated valves on floor-mount and wall-hung bowls, and we set the supply stop so the valve delivers its full rated volume without splashing or running on. Sensor valves cut down on touch points.

    High-Efficiency Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    High-efficiency toilets flush on 1.28 gallons or less, against 3.5 gallons or more for toilets from before the early 1990s. Look for the WaterSense label on the box. We steer customers toward models with a MaP score of 800 grams or higher, because a cheap low-flow bowl that needs two flushes saves nothing. Dual-flush models add a lighter half flush.

    Customer-Supplied Toilet Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Bought the toilet yourself? Check the rough-in printed on the box first. We unbox it and look over the porcelain for shipping cracks before we pull your old one, since a hairline crack in the bowl or tank means it needs to go back to the store, not onto your floor. We bring the seal and stainless bolts.
