    Frequently Asked Questions About Plumbers for Frozen Pipes in Sioux Falls

    Thawing an accessible frozen pipe in Sioux Falls usually costs $150 to $400, and burst pipe repair typically runs $300 to $1,000. Freeze-proofing is cheaper. Insulation, heat cable and frost-free faucets usually cost less than one burst repair.

    Insulate exposed pipes in attics, crawlspaces and garages, and seal air leaks around them. Disconnect garden hoses before cold weather. On the coldest nights, let a faucet on an outside wall drip, open the cabinet doors under sinks on exterior walls, and keep the thermostat at the same setting overnight instead of turning it down.

    Many policies cover sudden water damage from a burst pipe, though coverage varies. Read your policy. Some policies exclude damage if the house was left unheated or the water was not shut off while you were away, and we can give you an invoice, photos and a description of the failure to support your claim.

    Keep cold air away from the pipe. Seal and insulate around exposed lines. Close vents, insulate exposed runs, install frost-free hose bibs and add heat cable where needed.

    Pipe location matters more than brand. Copper and galvanized split first. Metal pipe in cold spots splits most, and PEX often survives except at the fittings.

    A faucet that trickles or runs dry after a cold night is the most common sign. Frost on exposed pipe is another. You may also notice a toilet that will not refill, a bulge in a pipe, or a strange smell from a drain, and any of those means you should open the faucet and call before the line thaws on its own.

    Most thawing jobs take one to two hours. Burst repairs take one to four hours. Finished walls add time for opening and cleanup, and multiple splits add more.

    A short, exposed section usually thaws in 30 to 60 minutes. Hidden lines take longer. A frozen line inside an exterior wall or under a floor can take a few hours, especially if it is PEX or CPVC, which we thaw with gentler infrared or warm air instead of an electric thawing machine.
