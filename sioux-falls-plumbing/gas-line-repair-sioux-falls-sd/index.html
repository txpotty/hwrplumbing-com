    Gas Appliance Connector Repair in Sioux Falls, SD

    Weak flames or a smell near one appliance often point to the connector or shutoff behind it. Older uncoated brass connectors can crack at the flare nut, and they are no longer accepted by most codes or utilities. We replace them with coated stainless connectors. Each appliance also gets a shutoff you can reach and a sediment trap where required, and we check that the connector length and diameter match the appliance rating.

    Gas Leak Detection in Sioux Falls

    A rotten egg smell, hissing near a line, dead grass in a stripe across the yard or a gas meter that moves with everything off are the common leak signs. Trust your nose. We isolate sections of the system and use electronic combustible gas detectors to narrow the source, then confirm with soap solution at fittings, valves and unions. Once we find it, you get a written repair price before work starts.

    Cost of Gas Line Repair in Sioux Falls, SD

    Gas line repair prices in Sioux Falls, SD depend on where the leak is and how much pipe has to change. Tightening or replacing a single fitting or connector is usually a few hundred dollars. Replacing a buried line that needs trenching across the yard can run $1,000 to $5,000 or more, depending on length, digging conditions and whether the driveway or a patio is in the way.     Black Iron Pipe Repair in Sioux Falls, SD

    Black iron is the standard interior gas pipe in a lot of homes, and it lasts a long time indoors. Joints are the weak point. Years of vibration from a furnace or a house settling can loosen threaded fittings, and pipe in a damp garage or an exterior wall can rust from the outside in. Bad sections get replaced with new pipe. Outdoor runs get coated or painted after the repair so rust does not start again.

    Gas Permits and Inspections in Sioux Falls

    Repairs that change piping, and all new gas lines, usually need a permit and inspection from Sioux Falls building officials. We file the permit, schedule the inspection and are on site when the inspector checks the work and the test gauge. Inspection usually happens within a few days. Once it passes, the utility can restore service and we come back to relight appliances if needed.

    Propane Gas Line Service in Sioux Falls, SD

    Propane systems run at different pressures than natural gas and use a two-stage regulator setup between the tank and the house. Regulators fail too. Our work covers propane yard lines, regulator replacement and fitting repairs, plus a check that every appliance has its propane orifices installed. A natural gas burner running on propane will burn rich and soot up. We test the system the same way we test natural gas piping.
