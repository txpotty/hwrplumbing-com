    Curbless Shower Installation in Sioux Falls

    Curbless showers need the floor lowered in the shower area. A wood floor gets dropped joists or a recessed pan, while a slab gets cut and lowered so the floor flows straight in. Linear drains help a lot. Slope and waterproofing get planned before demo.

    Prefab Shower Unit Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Prefab units are the fastest route to a new shower. The base goes on mortar, the drain and valve get connected and the walls are fastened so they stay rigid. They suit guest baths well. Most installs finish in a day.

    Shower Valve and Fixture Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Pressure-balancing valves are the baseline for any new shower. Thermostatic valves hold a set temperature and add volume control, which works well with rain heads and handhelds. Either one protects against scalding. We help you choose based on how many outlets you plan.

    Shower Upgrades Without a Full Remodel in Sioux Falls

    Not every upgrade needs demolition. A new pan, a tub conversion or a new valve and trim can change the bathroom without a gut job. We price the options side by side. Most of these finish in a few days.

    Steam Shower Plumbing in Sioux Falls

    Steam showers need four things: a generator, a water supply, a drain line and an electrical circuit. We install the generator within the maker's distance limits, plumb its supply and drain and run the steam line to the head inside the enclosure. The circuit is an electrician's job. A sealed enclosure with a sloped ceiling keeps drips off you.

    Digital Shower Systems in Sioux Falls, SD

    Digital shower systems like U by Moen and Kohler DTV need a little more planning. The digital valve mounts in an accessible spot, often in a vanity or a closet, and needs low-voltage wiring to the controller plus a dedicated outlet near the valve. The outlet is an electrician's job. Our part is the plumbing.

    Custom Shower System Installation in Sioux Falls, SD

    Multi-head showers depend on valve capacity. A standard pressure-balance valve can feed one head, but rain heads, body jets and handhelds need a thermostatic valve with separate volume controls and supply lines sized up to 3/4 inch. We plan the layout first. The water heater gets checked for capacity too.

    Shower Pan Installation in Sioux Falls

    Shower pans fail quietly. Pinholes and loose drain clamps let water into the subfloor slowly, which is why tiled pans sit flooded for a full day before tile. We level prefab pans carefully. Either way, the slope runs to the drain.
